# Wave 66D — Source Notes

Namespace: **W66D**

## W66D-D01 — FDA MAUDE / MDR
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-reporting-mdr-how-report-medical-device-problems/mdr-data-files

Public MDR files contain suspected device-associated deaths, serious injuries and malfunctions and are updated on a published cadence.

## W66D-D02 — openFDA Device Event
Primary:
https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/event/

Machine-readable MAUDE-derived adverse-event data support device/manufacturer/problem searches.

## W66D-D03 — Mandatory MDR Reporting
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/mandatory-reporting-requirements-manufacturers-importers-and-device-user-facilities

Manufacturers generally report qualifying deaths, serious injuries and malfunctions within 30 calendar days; five-day reports apply to specified high-urgency circumstances.

## W66D-D04 — Medical Device Recall Database
Primary:
https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfRES/RESSimpleSearch.cfm

The database includes classified medical-device recalls and, for some actions since 2017, corrections/removals initiated before FDA review.

## W66D-D05 — Recall Posting Caveat
FDA states recall posting/create date is the date FDA classified the recall and can be later than the firm's initiation/customer communication.

## W66D-D06 — openFDA Device Recall/Enforcement
Primary:
https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/recall/
https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/enforcement/

Use machine-readable fields with source-specific lifecycle limitations.

## W66D-D07 — FDA Safety Communications
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-safety/safety-communications

FDA publishes dated analysis and clinical recommendations for current device-safety issues.

## W66D-D08 — Medical Device Shortages List
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-supply-chain-and-shortages/medical-device-shortages-list

The public list provides product codes, availability, estimated duration, reason and revision dates; FDA also publishes permanent discontinuances.

## W66D-D09 — Current 2026 Shortage Examples
FDA's June 2026 update includes ongoing shortages in oxygenators, biopsy needles, endoscopic vessel harvesting systems and other device categories.

Use only as source-validation examples.
