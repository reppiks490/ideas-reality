# Wave 45F — Source Notes

Namespace: **W45F**

## W45F-D01 — PDUFA VII Review Goals
Primary:
FDA PDUFA VII commitment/performance materials.

Current FY2023–FY2027 performance goals include standard and priority review timelines by submission class.

## W45F-D02 — Sponsor Disclosures
Application-specific PDUFA goal dates are often disclosed through issuer SEC filings/releases.

Treat sponsor disclosure as the public clock where FDA does not provide a universal live date feed.

## W45F-D03 — FDA Advisory Committees
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees

Use official meeting calendar, notices, briefing documents and public vote materials.

## W45F-D04 — Drugs@FDA / openFDA Drugs@FDA

https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cder/daf/

## W45F-D05 — FDA Complete Response Letters
Primary:
https://open.fda.gov/apis/transparency/completeresponseletters/

## W45F-D06 — First Generic Approvals
Primary:

Use FDA-defined first-generic status, not market-news inference.

## W45F-D07 — Orange Book
