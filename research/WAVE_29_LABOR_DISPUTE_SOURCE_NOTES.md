# Wave 29L — Source Notes

Namespace: **W29L**

## W29L-D01 — FMCS F-7 Notices
Primary:
https://www.fmcs.gov/resources/documents-and-data/
https://www.fmcs.gov/resources/forms-applications/notice-of-bargaining-f-7/

FMCS publishes downloadable monthly F-7 notices. 2026 monthly files have generally appeared shortly after month-end.

## W29L-D02 — FMCS Notice Timing
FMCS explains that for covered CBA modification/termination, a party generally notifies the other party at least 60 days before expiration and, within 30 days after that notice, notifies FMCS/state mediation agencies. Healthcare timing is longer.

Use legal guidance as source semantics, not legal advice.

## W29L-D03 — NLRB Advanced Data Search
Primary:
https://www.nlrb.gov/advanced-search

NxGen data allow downloadable case/election datasets including case type, filed date, employer, union, allegations, unit/voter information and status.

## W29L-D04 — NLRB Posting Latency
NLRB's 2026 Chief FOIA Officer report says new cases filed with regional offices are posted to the website within about a day.

## W29L-D05 — National Mediation Board
Primary:
https://nmb.gov/

NMB administers collective-bargaining mediation under the Railway Labor Act for airline and railroad industries.

## W29L-D06 — NMB Cooling-Off
Primary:
https://nmb.gov/NMB_Application/index.php/meditation-overview-faq/

NMB states that after release from mediation following rejected arbitration, a 30-day cooling-off period begins; absent agreement/PEB, parties may then exercise self-help.

## W29L-D07 — Presidential Emergency Boards
Primary:
https://nmb.gov/NMB_Application/index.php/presidential-emergency-boards/

A Section 10 PEB generally delays self-help through a 30-day investigation/report period and another 30-day cooling-off period.

## W29L-D08 — NMB Public Releases
NMB publishes event-specific press releases confirming releases from mediation and exact self-help eligibility dates for airline/rail disputes.

## W29L-D09 — BLS Major Work Stoppages
Primary:
https://www.bls.gov/wsp/

BLS monthly data cover stoppages involving at least 1,000 workers and one full shift, with preliminary prior-month releases. Use as retrospective confirmation.
