# Wave 68R — Source Notes

Namespace: **W68R**

## W68R-D01 — 2026/2027 RFS Final Rule
Primary:
https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard/final-renewable-fuel-standards-2026-and-2027

EPA finalized 2026/2027 renewable-fuel standards on March 27, 2026. The rule includes 70% reallocation of SRE volumes from 2023–2025 and removes renewable electricity/eRINs as qualifying renewable fuel.

## W68R-D02 — 2026/2027 Applicable Volumes
Under the final rule, total applicable 2026 volumes include:
1.36 billion cellulosic RINs,
9.07 billion BBD RINs including reallocation,
11.10 billion advanced RINs including reallocation,
26.81 billion total renewable RINs including reallocation.

2027 applicable totals are:
1.43,
9.20,
11.32,
27.02 billion RINs respectively.

Use the final-rule table, not prior proposal values.

## W68R-D04 — August 31, 2026 SRE Action
Primary:
https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard/august-31-2026-decisions-petitions-rfs-small-refinery-exemptions

EPA decided 34 petitions for compliance year 2025:
18 full,
11 partial,
3 denied,
2 ineligible.

EPA separately stated the action exempted 1.76 billion 2025 RINs for 29 small refineries and that it intended to propose additional reallocation into 2026/2027 obligations.
