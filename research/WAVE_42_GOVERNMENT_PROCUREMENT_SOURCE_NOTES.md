## W42G-D03 — FAR 33.104
Primary:
https://www.acquisition.gov/far/33.104

For qualifying post-award GAO protests, the contracting officer must suspend performance or terminate the awarded contract when notice arrives within the rule-defined timing window, subject to authorized override.
