# Wave 51B — Source Notes

Namespace: **W51B**

## W51B-D01 — PACER
Primary:
https://pacer.uscourts.gov/
https://www.uscourts.gov/court-records/find-a-case-pacer

PACER provides federal bankruptcy docket/case documents. Specific-court records can be accessed in real time; the nationwide Case Locator is updated daily.

## W51B-D02 — CM/ECF
Primary:
https://www.uscourts.gov/court-records/electronic-filing-cm-ecf

CM/ECF is the federal Judiciary filing system for petitions, motions and other case documents.

## W51B-D03 — U.S. Courts Chapter 11 Basics
Primary:
https://www.uscourts.gov/court-programs/bankruptcy/bankruptcy-basics/chapter-11-bankruptcy-basics

Official overview covers debtor-in-possession operation, cash collateral, new borrowing and plan confirmation.

## W51B-D04 — U.S. Trustee Program
Primary:
https://www.justice.gov/ust/

Use current UST Chapter 11 manuals/guidelines for process and cash-collateral/DIP administration context.

## W51B-D05 — SEC Form 8-K Item 1.03
Primary:
SEC EDGAR current reports.

Listed/SEC-reporting debtors frequently disclose bankruptcy/receivership events under Item 1.03 and describe first-day financing/operational relief.

## W51B-D06 — SEC EDGAR Data APIs
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces

data.sec.gov submissions history is public JSON and updates in real time as filings are disseminated.

## W51B-D07 — Bankruptcy Code
Primary:
11 U.S.C. and controlling court orders.

Relevant sections include cash collateral/property use, postpetition financing, asset sales and plan confirmation.

## W51B-D08 — Claims / Notice Agents
Case-specific agents such as Kroll, Epiq and Stretto can publish public docket/claims material.

Treat as case-specific secondary mirrors, not complete national court records.

## W51B-D09 — RECAP / CourtListener
RECAP may provide accessible copies of PACER documents and docket metadata.

Coverage is opportunistic/incomplete; use only with explicit completeness flags.

## W51B-D10 — Evidence Boundary
Bankruptcy event studies/recovery literature can motivate mechanisms, but current research must reconstruct legal priority, liquidity and court timing case by case.
