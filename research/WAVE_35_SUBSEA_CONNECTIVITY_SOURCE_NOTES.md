# Wave 35C — Source Notes

Namespace: **W35C**

## W35C-D01 — FCC NORS
Primary:
https://www.fcc.gov/network-outage-reporting-system-nors

Qualifying communications outages are reported to NORS, including submarine-cable reporting under applicable rules. NORS data are presumed confidential and unavailable as a routine public signal.

## W35C-D02 — FCC Submarine Cable Landing Licenses
Primary:
https://www.fcc.gov/research-reports/guides/submarine-cable-landing-licenses
https://www.fcc.gov/submarine-cable-landing-licenses-granted

Public licensing data provide cable systems, applicants/licensees and U.S. landing information. FCC reported about 90 licensed systems operating or planned as of March 2026.

## W35C-D03 — FCC Circuit Capacity Data
Primary:
https://www.fcc.gov/international/circuit-capacity-data-us-international-submarine-cables

FCC publishes compiled U.S.-international submarine-cable capacity and landing-point information. Some individual filings/data may be withheld under confidential-treatment requests.

## W35C-D04 — Public Cable Operator Notices
Use first-party cable/carrier incident and repair notices as the preferred live confirmation source.
Example class: Vocus public Australia-Singapore Cable repair updates with fault location, temporary routing, vessel sequence, permitting and ETR.

## W35C-D05 — RIPE RIS Live
Reuse W34D-D07 for BGP route/path changes around physical-cable incidents.

## W35C-D06 — Georgia Tech IODA
Reuse W34D-D06 for independent ASN/region connectivity effects.

## W35C-D07 — Cloudflare Radar
Reuse W34D-D05 for Internet traffic anomalies/outages where relevant.

## W35C-D08 — Maritime Safety Notices
Official NAVAREA/NAVTEX/Local Notice to Mariners sources may identify cable-laying or repair vessel operations, coordinates and operating windows.
Use the issuing maritime authority where available rather than an unsourced aggregation.

## W35C-D09 — TeleGeography Submarine Cable Map
https://www.submarinecablemap.com/

Free public structural reference for cable systems, landing points, owners and ready-for-service dates. Respect site/data licensing and do not assume a public bulk API.

## W35C-D10 — Cable-Repair Mechanism Evidence
Industry/operator material shows repairs depend on fault localization, spares, specialized vessels, transit, permits, weather, cable recovery, splicing/testing and re-lay.
Use as mechanism evidence; incident state requires point-in-time public evidence.
