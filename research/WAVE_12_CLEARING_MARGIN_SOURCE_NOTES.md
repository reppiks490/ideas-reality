https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/clearing.html

## W12C-D02 — CME SPAN / SPAN2
https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/risk-management/performance-bonds-margins/span-methodology-overview.html
https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/risk-management/performance-bonds-margins/span-reference-documents.html

SPAN risk parameters include price/volatility scan ranges, spread parameters, delivery risk and short-option minimums. Legacy SPAN files historically had freely available download paths; current SPAN2 RPF workflows may require CME SFTP/service access. Verify entitlement at implementation time.

## W12C-D03 — CME Margin Services
https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/risk-management/margin-services.html

CME CORE/Margin Service and deployable software can calculate portfolio initial margin. Some services/licensing require CME agreements.

## W12C-D04 — CME Metals Issues and Stops

## W12C-D05 — CME Metal Depository Stocks
https://www.cmegroup.com/solutions/clearing/operations-and-deliveries/nymex-delivery-notices.html

COMEX-approved depositories report daily registered/eligible inventory. CME educational/regulatory materials document received, withdrawn, adjustment and total fields; gold reporting can include pledged-performance-bond inventory.

## W12C-D06 — CFTC Bank Participation Report
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/BankParticipationReports/index.htm
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/BankParticipationReports/ExplanatoryNotes/index.htm

Monthly. Data usually as of first Tuesday and released first Friday after 3:30 p.m. ET. Separate futures and gross-options reports; aggregate U.S. and non-U.S. bank positions.

## W12C-D07 — CME U.S. Equity Index Price Limits / DCBs
https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/equity-index/us-based-equity-index-futures-price-limits-faq.html
https://www.cmegroup.com/education/articles-and-reports/understanding-price-limits-and-circuit-breakers.html

NQ and other U.S. index products are subject to session-specific hard limits and dynamic circuit-breaker mechanics. Use current official daily limit tables and rules.
