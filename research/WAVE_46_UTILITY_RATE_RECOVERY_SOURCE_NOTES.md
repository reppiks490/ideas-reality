# Wave 46U — Source Notes

Namespace: **W46U**

## W46U-D01 — FERC eLibrary
Primary:
https://elibrary.ferc.gov/

Public filings/orders expose docket, accession, filed/posted timestamps, tariff/rate filings, formula-rate updates, settlements and Commission orders.

## W46U-D02 — FERC Formula Rates
Transmission owners publish annual formula-rate updates and true-ups under tariff protocols.

Use public supporting workpapers when available.

## W46U-D03 — Federal Power Act Section 205
Public-utility proposed rate/tariff changes and requested effective dates are filed publicly at FERC.

## W46U-D04 — Federal Power Act Section 206
FERC/complainants can challenge existing rates, leading to proceedings, refunds/adjustments and orders.

## W46U-D05 — California Public Utilities Commission
Primary:
https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/
https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/

Public proceedings/decisions provide rate-case, wildfire-cost, memorandum-account and securitization outcomes.

## W46U-D06 — State Public Utility Commissions
Other states maintain commission-specific public dockets/orders.

There is no assumed single national machine schema; connectors must be jurisdiction-specific.

## W46U-D07 — Wildfire / Storm Recovery Orders
Public utility decisions can explicitly authorize partial recovery and permanent shareholder disallowance.

Use exact order percentages/amounts.

## W46U-D08 — Recovery Bond / Financing Orders
Public commission financing orders specify approved recoverable costs, bond mechanics and customer charges.

## W46U-D09 — Utility SEC Filings
Issuer filings can provide requested/authorized amounts, regulatory assets, rate-case exposure and earnings impact.

Regulator orders control legal authorization.

## W46U-D10 — Existing Repo Cross-Sources
Reuse grid, wildfire, catastrophe-loss, transmission and outage waves for physical costs rather than duplicate them.
