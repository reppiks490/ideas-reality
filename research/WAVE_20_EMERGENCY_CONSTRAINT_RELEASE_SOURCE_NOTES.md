# Wave 20E — Source Notes

Namespace: **W20E**

## W20E-D01 — EPA Fuel Waivers
Primary:
https://www.epa.gov/gasoline-standards/fuel-waivers

EPA publishes current/historical temporary fuel waivers with scope, dates, geography and waived requirements.

## W20E-D02 — EPA Waiver Authority
Emergency waivers under Clean Air Act provisions require findings concerning extreme/unusual fuel supply circumstances and public interest. Use each original waiver letter for exact findings.

## W20E-D03 — FMCSA Emergency Declarations
Primary:
https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/emergency-declarations

Public declarations provide affected states/services, regulatory relief, effective/expiration dates and extensions.

## W20E-D04 — FMCSA Energy-Emergency Examples
2026 declarations document heating-fuel backlogs, propane shortages, rail disruption and refinery/power-outage effects, with HOS relief for direct-assistance carriers.

## W20E-D05 — DOE Section 202(c) Orders
Primary:
https://www.energy.gov/ceser/2026-doe-202c-orders

DOE publishes applications, orders and supporting documents for emergency power actions.

## W20E-D06 — 2026 PJM/ERCOT Orders
2026 orders include specified-resource dispatch authority, operating-limit relief and authorization of backup generation at large loads under defined reliability conditions.

## W20E-D07 — CBP Navigation-Law Waiver Requests
Primary:
https://www.cbp.gov/trade/jones-act-waiver-request

CBP states waiver requests must be published publicly upon receipt and specifies required cargo/vessel/port/date information.

## W20E-D08 — Jones Act Guidance
Primary:
https://www.cbp.gov/document/guidance/jones-act

Use current statutory/process guidance for eligibility, MARAD capacity review and time limits; historical rules must be versioned.

## W20E-D09 — CBP CSMS
CBP CSMS can publish implementation guidance for granted navigation-law waivers. Preserve message timestamp and scope.

## W20E-D10 — Existing Repo Cross-Sources
Fuse W20E with W14I infrastructure substitution, W15X industrial outages, W16S rail/customs throughput, grid telemetry, EIA inventories and physical logistics sources.
