# RDX06 — Sources

## D01 EIA-860M Preliminary Monthly Electric Generator Inventory
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/
Monthly proposed, operating, retired, canceled/postponed generator data. EIA states estimates are preliminary and can be corrected in later files without specific acknowledgment.

## D02 EIA-860M Survey Semantics
https://www.eia.gov/Survey/
Mandatory monthly update for proposed generators/changes within the survey horizon.

## D03 EIA Electric Power Monthly Capacity Tables
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/
Planned additions/retirements and realized retirements; useful validation, usually more lagged than 860M.

## D04 MISO Generator Interconnection Queue
https://www.misoenergy.org/planning/resource-utilization/generator-interconnection/
Public active project map/status, downloadable queue, commercial-operation dashboards and service-agreement context.

## D05 ERCOT Resource Adequacy / GIS
https://www.ercot.com/gridinfo/resource
Monthly Generator Interconnection Status Report and capacity trend data; 2026 series also adds reported transmission interconnection costs.

## D06 PJM Load Analysis Subcommittee
https://www.pjm.com/committees-and-groups/subcommittees/las
Public load forecast reports, large-load adjustment templates/reviews and data-center forecast methodology materials.

## D07 PJM Large Load
https://www.pjm.com/markets-and-operations/large-load
2026 framework/registry development distinguishes large-load commitments and bring-your-own-capacity concepts; implementation status must be versioned.

## D08 PJM 2026 Load Forecast
PJM explicitly distinguished near-term firm commitments from less-certain non-firm large-load projects, derating the latter.

## D09 FERC Large Load Docket RM26-4
https://www.ferc.gov/rm26-4
Federal proceedings on interconnection of large loads (>20 MW in the ANOPR context). Version final/approved rule state before production use.

## D10 Existing Grid Layers
Fuse with W23 transmission topology, grid telemetry, gas pipelines, utility regulatory/capacity-price research without duplicating connectors.
