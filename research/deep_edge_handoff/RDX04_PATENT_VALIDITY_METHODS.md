# RDX04 — Methods

1. Use P-TACTS public filing/document timestamps, not docket-event hindsight.
2. Track patent, claim and proceeding IDs separately.
3. Institution is a probability update, not invalidation.
4. Final written decisions are claim-specific; avoid patent-level binary labels.
5. Archive preliminary response, institution, FWD, Director Review, rehearing and appeal states.
6. Version 2026 Director Review rule changes, including the institution-review request deadline extension to 30 days.
7. Map patent to exact product/use only with Orange/Purple Book, litigation or issuer evidence.
8. Patent count is not barrier count; model independent blocking families/claims.
9. Separate patent expiration from FDA exclusivity expiration.
10. Orange Book is monthly: preserve each monthly vintage.
11. Paragraph IV submission/public-list date and private notice/litigation dates are different clocks.
12. Model the 30-month stay only when statutory conditions and timely suit are verified.
13. First-applicant 180-day exclusivity can remain a barrier after brand patent resolution.
14. Purple Book patent listing is sponsor-supplied and ministerial; not FDA validity review.
15. Preserve BLA/NDA/product/strength/indication mapping.
16. Control concurrent FDA approvals, clinical data, settlements and court rulings.
17. Model competitor readiness and capacity before translating legal win into price erosion.
18. Use revenue concentration and remaining protected indications point-in-time.
19. Negative control: challenged patents not materially linked to product economics.
20. Before market alpha require OOS prediction of institution/outcome, entry-timing revision, approval/launch, or measurable competitive erosion.
