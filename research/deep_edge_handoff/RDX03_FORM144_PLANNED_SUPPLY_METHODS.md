# RDX03 — Methods

1. Use EDGAR accepted timestamp as public-information clock.
2. Treat Form 144 as bona fide intent/proposed sale, not guaranteed execution.
3. Rule 144(h) requires filing concurrent with broker order/direct market-maker execution; model same-day hazard explicitly.
4. Use point-in-time shares outstanding/float and ADV.
5. Preserve amended Form 144 filings and broker changes.
6. Aggregate persons whose sales must be aggregated under Rule 144 rather than double-counting.
7. Keep 10b5-1 plan-adoption date distinct from filing and sale dates.
8. Control for compensation vesting, option exercises, tax withholding and scheduled plan cadence.
9. Link later Form 4 transactions with date/amount/price tolerance and seller identity confidence.
10. Absence of Form 4 match is not proof no sale if reporting/entity mapping differs.
11. Use uncertainty bands for unexecuted planned amount after filing.
12. Reconstruct applicable Rule 144 volume limits point-in-time.
13. Avoid using current float/ADV to evaluate historical filings.
14. Distinguish intraday, after-hours and premarket EDGAR acceptance.
15. Control earnings, major news, index rebalances and option expiry.
16. Separate price impact from information content: affiliate may sell for diversification/tax/charity.
17. Use matched seller/issuer controls and seller fixed effects.
18. Test large-filer effects nonlinearly versus ADV/float.
19. Do not equate broker identity with execution venue or beneficial owner beyond filing semantics.
20. Before promotion require OOS prediction of realized sale probability, sale size/timing, liquidity impact or ownership change.
