# RDX08 — Methods

1. Permit submitted, approved and status dates are separate.
2. Permit count is not production; learn conversion probabilities.
3. Use API/well identifiers and version operator/ownership changes.
4. Archive nightly state-source vintages prospectively.
5. Texas completion-form files represent forms submitted/approved the prior day, not necessarily physical completion that day.
6. FracFocus publication date can lag fracture date; learn publication-lag distribution.
7. Production data are delayed confirmation; never leak later monthly production into earlier features.
8. Separate new completion, recompletion, workover and test reports.
9. Model federal APDs separately from state/private permits.
10. APD validity/extensions create option life; approved-and-available does not guarantee drilling.
11. Tight-hole/confidential status creates informative missingness but not a directional signal by itself.
12. Normalize by rig count/service capacity and seasonality.
13. Learn operator-specific execution rates with shrinkage for small samples.
14. Type curves must be point-in-time and basin/formation/lateral specific.
15. Account legacy decline when translating new wells into net supply.
16. Include associated gas/NGL coproducts.
17. Takeaway/processing capacity can censor realized production.
18. Separate regulatory flare/vent permission from measured flaring.
19. Use later production as target/validation only at actual release time.
20. Before price alpha require OOS prediction of first-production timing, production volume, basin net growth or local basis.
