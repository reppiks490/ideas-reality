# RDX07 — Sources

## D01 FAA Releasable Aircraft Database
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/releasable_aircraft_download
Daily ~11:30 p.m. Central refresh; includes Master, Document Index, deregistered aircraft, make/model references and other files.

## D02 Aircraft Document Index
FAA download documentation describes fields including collateral/N-number, party, document ID, document receipt date, processing date, correction date and document type.

## D03 Document Index Inquiry
https://registry.faa.gov/aircraftinquiry/Search/DocumentIndexInquiry
Displays receipts for last 180 days and updates each federal working day at midnight.

## D04 FAA Registration Status
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/registration_status
Defines Valid, Sale Reported, Registration Pending and operational implications.

## D05 FAA Aircraft Registry
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry
FAA explicitly directs users to Document Index to verify receipt of registration documents and publishes current processing-date/backlog information.

## D06 Export Registration Cancellation
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/export_aircraft
Export requests identify N-number, manufacturer, model, serial and destination country.

## D07 Imported Aircraft
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/import_aircraft
Imported aircraft require foreign deregistration evidence, ownership evidence and U.S. registration process.

## D08 Deregistration
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/cancel_aircraft_registration
Covers export separately plus destroyed/dismantled/permanently retired/other cancellation.

## D09 FAA AVInfo
https://www.faa.gov/av-info
Public operator/aircraft/accident/service-difficulty categories can support operator and maintenance-state validation.

## D10 Data-Lag Warning
FAA notes ordered aircraft records can be incomplete while documents are in processing. Treat receipt, processing and final registration as separate clocks.
