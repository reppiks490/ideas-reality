# RDX04 — Patent Validity & Competitive-Entry Clock

Thesis: patent risk becomes economically important only when it changes the probability or timing of competitive entry. PTAB proceedings provide public legal state transitions; FDA Orange Book, Purple Book and Paragraph IV data define whether those patent outcomes actually remove a barrier to generic/biosimilar competition.

## RDX04-E01 — IPR Petition Shock
Public P-TACTS petition filing against a patent mapped to a material product.

## RDX04-E02 — Petition Materiality
Patent-linked product revenue/exposure × challenged claim coverage × remaining patent life.

## RDX04-E03 — Preliminary-Response Clock
Patent owner generally has three months after the filing-date notice to file a preliminary response.

## RDX04-E04 — Institution-Decision Clock
35 USC 314(b): institution determination within three months after preliminary response or its deadline.

## RDX04-E05 — Institution Probability
Estimate P(institution) from petition grounds, prior art, claim construction, discretionary factors and historical PTAB state.

## RDX04-E06 — Institution Surprise
Actual institution/denial minus ex-ante institution probability.

## RDX04-E07 — Claim-Scope Institution
Fraction/economic importance of product-protecting claims actually put into trial.

## RDX04-E08 — Institution != Invalidation
Treat institution only as a validity-risk update; never mark claims invalid at institution.

## RDX04-E09 — Final-Written-Decision Clock
If instituted and not dismissed, PTAB ordinarily issues final determination within one year of institution, extendable by six months for good cause.

## RDX04-E10 — Final Claim Survival Tensor
For each challenged claim: upheld / unpatentable / disclaimed / settled/no final resolution.

## RDX04-E11 — Product Protection Remaining After FWD
Recompute product-level exclusivity after claim-level outcome across all relevant patents.

## RDX04-E12 — Patent-Family Redundancy
One patent loss may have little effect if other independently blocking patents remain.

## RDX04-E13 — Weakest-Link Patent
Identify patents whose loss meaningfully moves earliest lawful competitive-entry date.

## RDX04-E14 — Patent-Thicket Resilience
Estimate number and independence of remaining blocking patent families, not raw patent count.

## RDX04-E15 — Director Review State
Track request / granted / denied / sua sponte / resolved states.

## RDX04-E16 — 2026 Director-Review Institution Clock
USPTO currently extends request deadline for institution decisions from 14 to 30 days; version this rule historically.

## RDX04-E17 — Director-Review Reversal Hazard
Estimate probability that institution/FWD posture changes under Director Review.

## RDX04-E18 — Appeal Clock
Track Federal Circuit notice, briefing, decision and remand only at public availability.

## RDX04-E19 — Parallel District-Court State
PTAB and infringement litigation interact; preserve stay, Markman, summary judgment, trial and appeal as separate public events.

## RDX04-E20 — PTAB-to-Settlement Hazard
Institution can change litigation bargaining power even before final written decision.

## RDX04-E21 — Orange Book Patent Barrier Map
Monthly point-in-time patent number, expiration, use-code, drug-product/substance flags and delist-request state by NDA/product.

## RDX04-E22 — Paragraph IV Competitive-Entry Signal
FDA regularly publishes drugs with substantially complete ANDAs containing Paragraph IV challenges.

## RDX04-E23 — First PIV Submission Clock
Use FDA's date of first substantially complete PIV submission as earliest public challenge-state field when published.

## RDX04-E24 — 30-Month Stay State
If timely infringement suit follows Paragraph IV notice, generic approval is generally postponed for 30 months unless earlier patent resolution/expiration applies.

## RDX04-E25 — First-Applicant 180-Day Exclusivity State
Track eligibility, forfeiture/relinquishment and first commercial marketing because this can delay other generic entrants even after brand patent risk falls.

## RDX04-E26 — Patent Delist Paradox
Orange Book patents can remain listed with delist-request flag while first-applicant 180-day exclusivity issues remain unresolved.

## RDX04-E27 — Generic Entry Date Distribution
Compute low/base/high earliest competitive-entry timing from patent, exclusivity, stay, FDA approval and first-applicant states.

## RDX04-E28 — Purple Book Biologic Patent Map
Use sponsor-provided biologic patent list, BLA/reference product and patent expiration.

## RDX04-E29 — Purple Book Ministerial Boundary
FDA states it publishes sponsor-supplied biologic patent information ministerially and does not determine validity/enforceability/infringement. Never treat listing as patent-strength validation.

## RDX04-E30 — Biologic Reference-Product Exclusivity Clock
Reference-product exclusivity generally blocks effective 351(k) approval until 12 years after first licensure, plus applicable pediatric extension.

## RDX04-E31 — Biosimilar Patent-vs-Exclusivity Gap
A patent win does not create entry if statutory/orphan exclusivity still blocks licensure.

## RDX04-E32 — Biosimilar Approval-Ready State
Map licensed biosimilar/interchangeable products and remaining legal barriers to actual commercial entry.

## RDX04-E33 — Competitive-Entry Acceleration
Change in expected earliest generic/biosimilar launch date after PTAB/court/FDA event.

## RDX04-E34 — Revenue-at-Risk Duration
Product revenue exposure × acceleration in expected competition timing.

## RDX04-E35 — Competitor Readiness
Patent barrier removal matters more if a generic/biosimilar applicant is already filed/approved/manufacturing-ready.

## RDX04-E36 — Competitive Capacity
Estimate likely number/quality of entrants and substitute supply, not binary generic/no generic.

## RDX04-E37 — Patent Outcome × Pricing Erosion
Translate entry timing into expected price/share erosion by therapeutic/product market structure.

## RDX04-E38 — PTAB Event Market-Reaction Residual
Actual market reaction minus expected reaction conditioned on product materiality, patent redundancy, claim scope and competitor readiness.

## RDX04-E39 — Validity Reality Gap
Market prices patent loss as material while remaining exclusivity/patent barriers imply little change—or the inverse.

## RDX04-E40 — Patent-to-Competition Truth Ladder
PETITION -> INSTITUTION -> CLAIM OUTCOME -> REVIEW/APPEAL -> REMAINING PATENT/EXCLUSIVITY BARRIERS -> FDA-READY COMPETITOR -> COMMERCIAL ENTRY -> PRICE/SHARE EROSION -> EARNINGS.
