# RDX02 — Sources

## D01 openFDA Device Adverse Events
https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/event/
Source is MAUDE. openFDA states coverage through current 2026 data with weekly API updates.

## D02 FDA MAUDE Data Files
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-reporting-mdr-how-report-medical-device-problems/mdr-data-files
Bulk public MAUDE files are updated monthly; openFDA provides a faster weekly machine-readable layer.

## D03 Mandatory Device Reporting
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/mandatory-reporting-requirements-manufacturers-importers-and-device-user-facilities
Manufacturers: 30-day reports for qualifying deaths/serious injuries/malfunctions; 5-work-day reports for designated events or remedial-action events needed to prevent unreasonable risk of substantial harm.

## D04 Medical Device Early Alerts / Recalls
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-safety/medical-device-recalls-and-early-alerts
FDA states Early Alerts can describe company actions likely to be the most serious recalls before FDA has determined that they meet the regulatory definition of recall.

## D05 Safety Communications
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-safety/safety-communications

## D06 Corrections and Removals — 21 CFR Part 806
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/recalls-corrections-and-removals-devices
Risk-to-health corrections/removals generally must be reported within 10 working days of initiation; scope extensions require amendments.

## D07 Medical Device Recall Database
https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfRES/RESSimpleSearch.cfm
FDA notes the database can include firm-initiated correction/removal actions before FDA review/classification. Database cadence is slower than MAUDE/openFDA.

## D08 Device Shortage / Discontinuance Lists
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-supply-chain-and-shortages/medical-device-shortages-list
Public list includes current shortage status, expected duration, reasons and manufacturer discontinuances.

## D09 UDI / GUDID
https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/udi/
Weekly API updates support device-family/entity resolution.

## D10 FDA Device Databases
https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/device-advice-comprehensive-regulatory-assistance/medical-device-databases
Use registration/listing, PMA, 510(k), PAS and classification sources as structural exposure metadata.
