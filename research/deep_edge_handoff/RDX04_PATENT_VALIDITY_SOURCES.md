# RDX04 — Sources

## D01 USPTO P-TACTS
https://ptacts.uspto.gov/ptacts/ui/public-search
All public AIA Review cases/documents can be searched without registration by case, patent, application, party and proceeding type.

## D02 Inter Partes Review
https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/trials/inter-partes-review
IPR institution threshold is reasonable likelihood petitioner prevails on at least one challenged claim. If instituted and not dismissed, final determination is normally within one year, extendable six months for good cause.

## D03 35 USC 314(b)
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=%28title%3A35+section%3A314+edition%3Aprelim%29
Institution determination is due within three months after preliminary response or the deadline for one.

## D04 PTAB Trial Practice Guide
https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/trial-practice-guide
Patent owner preliminary response generally due within three months after filing-date notice; contains current PTAB practice guidance.

## D05 Director Review
https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/decisions/director-review-process
Current 2026 procedure; institution-decision Director Review request deadline is presently extended to 30 days under June 2026 precedential guidance.

## D06 PTAB Statistics
https://www.uspto.gov/patents/ptab/statistics
FY2026 trial statistics and Director Review request spreadsheet.

## D07 FDA Orange Book Files
https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-approvals-and-databases/orange-book-data-files
Monthly downloadable product, patent and exclusivity data including patent expiration/use codes/delist flags.

## D08 FDA Paragraph IV List
https://www.fda.gov/drugs/abbreviated-new-drug-application-anda/patent-certifications-and-suitability-petitions
Regularly published PIV challenge data include first submission date, number of ANDAs, 180-day status and related fields. Timely patent suit can generally cause a 30-month stay.

## D09 FDA Purple Book
https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/
Includes biologic products, biosimilar/interchangeable relationships and exclusivity information.

## D10 Purple Book Patent List
https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/index.cfm?event=patentlist
Patent list is sponsor-supplied; FDA's role is ministerial and it does not opine on validity/enforceability/infringement.
