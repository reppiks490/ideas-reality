# RDX03 — Sources

## D01 SEC Rule 144
https://www.sec.gov/reports/rule-144-selling-restricted-control-securities
Affiliates must file Form 144 when proposed Rule 144 sales in a three-month period exceed 5,000 shares or $50,000 aggregate sale price, subject to rule conditions.

## D02 17 CFR 230.144(h)
https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/17/230.144
The notice is filed concurrently with placing a broker sale order or direct market-maker execution, and the filer must have a bona fide intention to sell within a reasonable time.

## D03 Form 144
https://www.sec.gov/forms/form144.pdf
Fields include shares/units proposed, aggregate market value, shares outstanding, approximate sale date, intended exchange, broker/market maker, acquisition history, prior 3-month sales and 10b5-1 plan-adoption/instruction date.

## D04 SEC Form 144 XML
https://www.sec.gov/files/edgar/filer-information/specifications/form144xmltechspec1-0
Electronic structured filing enables machine parsing.

## D05 EDGAR Acceptance Time
Form 144 filing indexes expose exact acceptance timestamps and issuer/reporting-person identities.

## D06 SEC Corporation Finance Interpretations
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/corporation-finance-interpretations-cfis/consolidated-corporation-finance-interpretations
SEC guidance confirms same-day concurrence semantics and amendment treatment.
