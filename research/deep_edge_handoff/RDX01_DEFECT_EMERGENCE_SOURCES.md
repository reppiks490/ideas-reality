# RDX01 — Sources

## D01 NHTSA Consumer Complaints
https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis
NHTSA labels complaint data daily and provides API/bulk access.

## D02 NHTSA Manufacturer Communications
https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-manufacturers/manufacturer-communications
https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis
Manufacturer communications cover defects/failures/malfunctions, including non-safety issues. Reporting obligation is monthly; public bulk files can be refreshed more frequently.

## D03 NHTSA Recalls / Part 573
https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis
Recall dataset is daily. NHTSA states manufacturers must report within five business days after determining a safety defect/noncompliance.

## D04 NHTSA Investigations
https://www.nhtsa.gov/search-safety-issues
https://www.nhtsa.gov/resources-investigations-recalls

## D05 Early Warning Reporting
https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-manufacturers/early-warning-reporting
2026 schedule: quarterly aggregates, monthly manufacturer communications, foreign recall reporting within five days of determination.

## D06 Recall Completion
https://www.nhtsa.gov/resources-related-investigations-and-recalls/annual-recall-reports

## D07 Public-data boundary
Some EWR categories can be confidential/delayed. Verify field-level availability before implementation.

## D08 Stop-sale evidence
NHTSA-hosted manufacturer communications can explicitly identify affected new dealer inventory subject to stop-sale. Use campaign-specific evidence only.

## D09 Part 573 source documents
Public defect-information reports provide affected population, chronology, defect description and remedy.

## D10 Empirical mechanism evidence
Recent peer-reviewed work documents negative average automaker recall reactions and supplier spillovers conditioned by investigation duration, recall traits and buyer dependence. Reproduce independently.
