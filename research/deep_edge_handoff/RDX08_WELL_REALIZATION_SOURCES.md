# RDX08 — Sources

## D01 Texas RRC Oil & Gas Queries
https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/ewaMain.do
Drilling Permit, Wellbore, Production and related public queries.

## D02 Texas Drilling Permit W-1
https://webapps2.rrc.texas.gov/EWA/drillingPermitsQueryAction.do
Searchable by submitted/approved date, operator, API, field, well type, status and completion status.

## D03 Texas Downloadable Data
https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/data-sets-available-for-download/
Full Wellbore weekly; Wellbore Query monthly; **Completion Information nightly**, containing completion forms submitted or approved the prior day.

## D04 Texas Research Queries
https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/
Completions and drilling permits are listed as nightly; Wellbore Query data are updated daily.

## D05 Texas Production
https://www.rrc.texas.gov/oil-and-gas/research-and-statistics/production-data/
Monthly production is delayed and should serve as confirmation/target, not live milestone.

## D06 BLM Applications for Permit to Drill
https://www.blm.gov/programs/energy-and-minerals/oil-and-gas/operations-and-production/permitting/applications-permits-drill
Publishes pending, processed, approved and approved-available-to-drill APD inventories. Approved APDs are typically valid two years and may be extended.

## D07 FracFocus
https://fracfocus.org/data-download
Public disclosure download updated five days/week. Treat disclosure/publication time separately from reported fracture date.

## D08 North Dakota DMR Daily Activity
https://www.dmr.nd.gov/oilgas/dailyindex.asp
Daily activity reports include permits and other current well activity; state methodology differs from Texas.

## D09 North Dakota Public GIS
State FeatureServer layers expose permit/well states and can support machine-readable monitoring.

## D10 Existing Pipeline/Inventory Layers
Fuse with pipeline, storage, refinery, gas-transport and physical-delivery research already in ideas-reality rather than duplicating connectors.
