# RDX03 — Form 144 Planned Supply & Insider Sale Intent

Thesis: Form 4 tells you what insiders already transacted. Form 144 can reveal a proposed Rule 144 sale at the moment the sale order is being placed. For qualifying affiliate sales, the filer must submit Form 144 electronically and transmit it concurrently with placing the broker order or executing directly with a market maker. The form exposes proposed shares, aggregate market value, approximate sale date, exchange/broker, acquisition history, prior three-month sales and 10b5-1 plan-adoption date where applicable.

The edge is not "insider selling is bearish." It is the amount of prospective control-stock supply relative to liquidity, prior selling, float, plan status and market state.

## RDX03-E01 — Proposed Sale Notional
Number of shares proposed × filing-time price.

## RDX03-E02 — Proposed Sale / ADV
Planned shares divided by trailing point-in-time average daily volume.

## RDX03-E03 — Proposed Sale / Float
Planned shares divided by point-in-time public float/shares outstanding.

## RDX03-E04 — Rule 144 Capacity Utilization
Compare proposed amount with applicable Rule 144 volume limit.

## RDX03-E05 — Approximate Sale-Date Clock
Use the form's approximate date of sale as the planned supply window, not merely filing date.

## RDX03-E06 — Same-Day Execution Hazard
Because filing is concurrent with order placement/execution under Rule 144(h), model immediate execution probability after acceptance time.

## RDX03-E07 — After-Hours Filing / Next-Session Supply
Separate filings accepted after regular close from intraday filings.

## RDX03-E08 — 10b5-1 vs Discretionary Intent
Use disclosed plan-adoption date. Separate prearranged plan sales from non-10b5-1 filings.

## RDX03-E09 — Plan Age
Days from 10b5-1 plan adoption/instruction to Form 144 filing.

## RDX03-E10 — Newly Adopted vs Mature Plan
Short plan age may carry different informational/flow content from long-standing plans, subject to applicable cooling-off rules.

## RDX03-E11 — Prior 3-Month Sale Velocity
Form 144 Table II reports prior three-month sales by the filer/aggregated persons.

## RDX03-E12 — Supply Acceleration
Current proposed shares relative to prior three-month realized sale pace.

## RDX03-E13 — Repeat-Filer Cadence
Estimate recurring Form 144 schedule by person/issuer and detect abnormal size/timing.

## RDX03-E14 — Executive Role Weight
CEO/CFO/founder/director/10% holder/other affiliate may have different sale motives and market interpretation.

## RDX03-E15 — Founder/Controller Supply State
Large control holders can create persistent float expansion. Track cumulative proposed/realized sales.

## RDX03-E16 — Acquisition-Nature Signal
Use Table I nature/date of acquisition to distinguish option exercise, grant, gift, purchase or other acquisition source where disclosed.

## RDX03-E17 — Compensation-Monetization State
Equity acquired through compensation/exercise and promptly proposed for sale may differ from sale of long-held founder/control stock.

## RDX03-E18 — Gift/Trust/Entity Routing
Aggregate related persons/entities required by Rule 144 semantics; avoid treating each filing as independent supply.

## RDX03-E19 — Broker Concentration
Track named broker/market-maker concentration for recurring large sellers.

## RDX03-E20 — Exchange/Venue Intent
Use named exchange where disclosed; validate against actual public market structure.

## RDX03-E21 — Planned Supply × Closing Liquidity
Proposed sale amount divided by expected auction/continuous liquidity for intended sale date.

## RDX03-E22 — Planned Supply × Gamma State
Large affiliate supply can have different impact under positive/negative dealer gamma.

## RDX03-E23 — Planned Supply × Short Constraint
Fuse with SSR/borrow/short crowding states. Supply can either relieve borrow scarcity or increase directional pressure.

## RDX03-E24 — Planned Supply × Index Rebalance
Collision between insider supply and forced benchmark demand/selling may amplify or absorb flow.

## RDX03-E25 — Planned Supply × Buyback Capacity
Compare insider/control-stock supply with issuer repurchase authorization/execution state where point-in-time public.

## RDX03-E26 — Filing-to-Form-4 Realization
Link Form 144 proposed sale to later Form 4 reported transaction where applicable.

## RDX03-E27 — Realization Ratio
Actual sale shares / proposed shares within the reasonable execution window.

## RDX03-E28 — Non-Realization Signal
Form 144 filed but little/no later realized sale: limit order not reached, cancellation, changed plan or mapping issue.

## RDX03-E29 — Proposed-vs-Realized Price Slippage
Compare approximate/filing price context with later Form 4 realized sale prices.

## RDX03-E30 — Sale Persistence Half-Life
Estimate how long proposed supply remains economically relevant after filing.

## RDX03-E31 — Insider Supply Breadth
Aggregate proposed sales across multiple affiliates of same issuer in a rolling window.

## RDX03-E32 — Executive Cluster Sale
Multiple executives/directors filing Form 144 around the same period, controlling for shared 10b5-1 schedules.

## RDX03-E33 — Control-Holder Overhang
Cumulative unexecuted/recent planned supply from large holders relative to float/ADV.

## RDX03-E34 — Planned Supply Surprise
Observed proposed sale minus expected sale from person's historical cadence, compensation cycle and plan.

## RDX03-E35 — Liquidity Absorption Quotient
LSAQ = expected near-term realized affiliate supply / adaptive executable liquidity.

## RDX03-E36 — Market Reaction Residual
Actual post-filing return/impact minus expectation conditioned on notional, plan status, seller role, liquidity and prior selling.

## RDX03-E37 — Form 144 / Form 4 Divergence
Planned sale signal versus later actual sale. Research whether market reacts to intent, execution, or both.

## RDX03-E38 — Form 144 / Insider-Buy Contradiction
Large planned affiliate sale occurring alongside discretionary Form 4 insider purchases by other insiders.

## RDX03-E39 — Supply-to-Float Regime
Classify LOW / NORMAL / HIGH / EXTREME planned control-stock supply pressure.

## RDX03-E40 — Planned Supply Truth Ladder
PLAN ADOPTION -> FORM 144 / BROKER ORDER -> EXECUTION -> FORM 4 -> FLOAT/LIQUIDITY IMPACT -> OWNERSHIP CHANGE -> PRICE/FINANCIAL EFFECT.
