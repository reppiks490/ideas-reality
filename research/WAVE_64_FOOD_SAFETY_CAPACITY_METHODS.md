# Wave 64F — Research Methods

Namespace: **W64F**

## W64F-M01 — Recall Public-Time Ledger
Archive initial notice and every update/termination at first public availability.

## W64F-M02 — Recall Is Voluntary
FSIS-regulated recalls are generally initiated by the firm/importer, sometimes at FSIS request; do not mislabel recall as inspection suspension.

## W64F-M03 — Public-Health Alert Separation
Alert and recall are distinct regulatory states.

## W64F-M04 — Poundage Revision
Recall pounds can change as scope develops; preserve every vintage.

## W64F-M05 — Establishment Identity
Use FSIS establishment number plus legal/name history.

## W64F-M06 — Directory Weekly Vintage
MPI directory/demographics are updated weekly and replace prior editions.
Archive each vintage.

## W64F-M07 — Enforcement Publication Lag
Quarterly enforcement reports are delayed snapshots.
Never backfill action details into event time.

## W64F-M08 — Action Semantics
Separate:
regulatory control,
withholding,
suspension,
withdrawal of inspection,
voluntary suspension.

## W64F-M09 — Partial-vs-Total Scope
Capacity impact requires process/plant scope.

## W64F-M10 — Production Denominator
Use species/product-specific plant throughput estimates with uncertainty.

## W64F-M11 — Slaughter Common-Cause Control
Animal supply shocks can independently reduce plant throughput.
Fuse/control W58A.

## W64F-M12 — Demand Common-Cause Control
Consumer avoidance after recall can reduce demand while plant capacity is unchanged.

## W64F-M13 — Product Inventory Buffer
Finished product/cold-storage inventory can cushion processing interruption.

## W64F-M14 — Reroute Feasibility
Alternative plant counts only if species, inspection, logistics and available line capacity match.

## W64F-M15 — Export Eligibility Versioning
Country-specific establishment eligibility can change.

## W64F-M16 — Multi-Recall Deduplication
Several product notices may share one root contamination event.

## W64F-M17 — Recovery Definition
Recall termination, inspection reinstatement and throughput normalization are separate.

## W64F-M18 — Small-Plant Weighting
Plant count is not capacity; weight by productive importance.

## W64F-M19 — Negative-Control Establishments
Unaffected nearby plants help distinguish market-wide animal/demand shocks.

## W64F-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Before financial alpha require OOS improvement in:
enforcement escalation,
plant capacity,
slaughter,
wholesale output,
spread,
or recovery.
