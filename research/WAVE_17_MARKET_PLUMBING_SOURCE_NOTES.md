# Wave 17P — Source Notes

Namespace: **W17P**

## W17P-D01 — Federal Reserve Financial Services Service Status
Primary:
https://www.frbservices.org/app/status/serviceStatus.do

Public page reports operational state across FedACH, FedNow, Fedwire Funds, Fedwire Securities, National Settlement and other FRFS services.

## W17P-D02 — FRFS Business Continuity Guides
Primary:
https://www.frbservices.org/resources/resource-centers/business-continuity/

Guides describe use of Service Status, contingency communications, operating extensions and reconciliation expectations.

## W17P-D03 — DTCC Operational Resilience
Primary:
https://www.dtcc.com/about/protecting-the-markets/operational-resilience

DTCC describes incident communication practices and client subscription channels. Treat those subscription communications separately from public notices.

## W17P-D04 — DTCC Important Notices
Primary:
https://www.dtcc.com/legal/important-notices

Public notices can document major clearing/settlement operational events and rule actions.

## W17P-D05 — June 2026 DTC/NSCC Settlement Rollover
Public DTCC notices dated June 30 / July 9, 2026 document the June 29 rollover, Federal Reserve settlement extensions, next-day completion and associated participant funding consequences.

Use as a real case study and test fixture.

## W17P-D06 — Nasdaq Market System Status
Primary:
https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=MarketSystemStatus

Nasdaq provides current status and searchable historical system-status messages.

## W17P-D07 — Cboe System Status
Primary:
https://www.cboe.com/system-status/
and asset-class-specific notice pages.

Use exact venue/service/status timestamps.

## W17P-D08 — Cboe/Nasdaq Operational Halt Rules
Exchange public rules/alerts describe operational-halt authority and behavior around system/SIP problems.

Version rule text historically.

## W17P-D09 — CME Globex Notices
Primary:
https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/electronic-trading.html

Useful mainly for planned migrations/configuration/market-access changes and product trading-status context. Do not assume it is a complete real-time incident feed.
