# Wave 59P — Research Methods

Namespace: **W59P**

## W59P-M01 — Quarantine Vintage Archive
Archive every published map/written-description version and public posting time.

## W59P-M02 — Legal Geometry First
Use legal quarantine descriptions where map rendering and text disagree.

## W59P-M03 — Host-Specific Intersection
Only host crops count toward affected productive acreage.

## W59P-M04 — Crop Acreage Vintage
Use point-in-time acreage/production geography available at event time.

## W59P-M05 — Restriction Semantics
Movement restrictions vary by article and treatment status.

## W59P-M06 — Detection vs Established Infestation
One detection is not equivalent to established population.

## W59P-M07 — Eradication Window
Use pest-specific trapping/treatment criteria for expected quarantine duration.

## W59P-M08 — Perennial Biological Lag
Tree crop recovery needs age/maturation model, not linear acreage recovery.

## W59P-M09 — Disease Prevalence Uncertainty
Quarantine area is not infection prevalence.

Keep exposure and confirmed disease distinct.

## W59P-M10 — Weather Common-Cause Control
Weather can both increase pest pressure and reduce yields.

## W59P-M11 — NASS Release Clock
Use scheduled public release time for crop forecasts.

## W59P-M12 — Forecast Revision Decomposition
Separate new field information from methodology/revision changes where possible.

## W59P-M13 — Trade Restriction Versioning
Destination phytosanitary rules can change independently of domestic quarantine.

## W59P-M14 — Export Flow Lag
Shipment/trade data arrive later than quarantine changes; preserve publication lag.

## W59P-M15 — Import Substitution Constraint
Imports require matching grade/product/season and sanitary access.

## W59P-M16 — Nursery Replacement Constraint
Track availability of clean/eligible nursery stock for perennial recovery.

## W59P-M17 — Quarantine-Lift Confirmation
Legal release does not imply immediate shipment normalization.

## W59P-M18 — Pest Identity Integrity
Do not pool fruit flies/citrus diseases/wood pests as one mechanism.

## W59P-M19 — Negative-Control Crops
Non-host crops inside same geography should show weaker effects.

## W59P-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Before financial testing require OOS improvement in:
affected acreage,
movement,
crop production revision,
shipment,
or recovery.
