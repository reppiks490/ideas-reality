# Wave 40B — Source Notes

Namespace: **W40B**

## W40B-D01 — FDA Import Alerts
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/industry/actions-enforcement/import-alerts

FDA states Import Alert databases are updated in real time and explains Red/Yellow/Green List DWPE semantics.

## W40B-D02 — FDA AccessData Import Alerts
Primary:
https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cms_ia/

Provides searchable alert pages by industry, country, alert number and publish date.

## W40B-D03 — FDA Import Refusals Dashboard
Primary:
https://datadashboard.fda.gov/oii/cd/imprefusals.htm

FDA states refusal data are updated weekly and include final actions. Treat as delayed confirmation.

## W40B-D04 — CBP WRO & Findings
Primary:
https://www.cbp.gov/document/stats/withhold-release-orders-findings

CBP publishes downloadable WRO/Finding data and updates it as actions are issued or modified.

## W40B-D05 — CBP WRO / Findings Dashboard
Primary:
https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/stats/trade/withhold-release-orders-findings-dashboard

CBP notes it does not generally publicize specific detention/disposition events under these orders.

## W40B-D06 — CBP Forced Labor Enforcement
Primary:
https://www.cbp.gov/trade/forced-labor/enforcement

CBP publishes aggregate forced-labor enforcement statistics and distinguishes WRO, Finding, UFLPA and CAATSA actions.

## W40B-D07 — DHS UFLPA Entity List
Primary:
DHS/FLETF UFLPA Entity List and Federal Register notices.

Archive every entity addition/removal/effective date.

## W40B-D08 — CBP UFLPA Enforcement Statistics
Use public dashboard data only at its published aggregation level; do not infer hidden shipment or company details.

## W40B-D09 — FDA Drug Shortages
Primary:
https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/drug-shortages

FDA updates current drug-shortage status and identifies manufacturing, raw-material and shipping delays among possible causes.
