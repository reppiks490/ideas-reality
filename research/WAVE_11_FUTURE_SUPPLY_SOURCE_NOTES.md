# Wave 11S — Primary Source Notes

Namespace: W11S

## W11S-D01 — Railroad Commission of Texas
https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/research-queries/
https://www.rrc.texas.gov/resource-center/research/data-sets-available-for-download/

RRC documents pending drilling-permit data updated twice daily, permit data nightly, completion query/data nightly, and production monthly.

## W11S-D02 — FracFocus
https://fracfocus.org/data-download

Public SQL/CSV disclosure download refreshed five days per week. Treat disclosure publication separately from physical fracture date.

## W11S-D03 — EIA-860M
https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia860m/

Monthly preliminary generator-specific inventory with operating, proposed, retired and postponed/canceled units.

## W11S-D04 — PJM Interconnection / New Services
https://www.pjm.com/planning/service-requests/interconnection-queues

Use public project identifiers and lifecycle/status data. Archive revisions.

## W11S-D05 — ERCOT Generator Interconnection
https://www.ercot.com/gridinfo/resource

Monthly Generator Interconnection Status reports and operational overviews expose project phase, fuel and planned capacity.

## W11S-D06 — FERC Major Pipeline Projects
https://www.ferc.gov/industries-data/natural-gas/major-pipeline-projects-pending
https://www.ferc.gov/industries-data/natural-gas/approved-major-pipeline-projects-1997-present
https://data.ferc.gov/nepa-schedule-for-pending-infrastructure-projects/

Project/docket capacity and regulatory milestones.

## W11S-D07 — EIA LNG / Natural Gas Projects
https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/data.php

EIA publishes U.S. liquefaction capacity/project status including existing, under-construction and approved projects.

## W11S-D08 — DOE LNG Export Authorizations
https://www.energy.gov/fecm/listings-lng-reports

Use authorization/status and semiannual construction reporting with explicit separation between authorization, FID, construction and operation.
