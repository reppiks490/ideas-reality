# Wave 64F — Source Notes

Namespace: **W64F**

## W64F-D01 — FSIS Recalls & Public Health Alerts
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts

FSIS publishes current recall/public-health notices for meat, poultry and egg products.

## W64F-D02 — Recall Semantics
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/understanding-fsis-food-recalls

FSIS describes recalls as voluntary company actions; establishments must notify FSIS after learning adulterated/misbranded product entered commerce.

## W64F-D03 — Quarterly Enforcement Reports
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/regulatory-enforcement/quarterly-enforcement-reports

Reports identify NOIE, withholding and suspension states by establishment but are quarterly snapshots.

## W64F-D04 — Enforcement Definitions
FSIS defines regulatory control, withholding and suspension distinctly; suspension interrupts inspector assignment in all or part of an establishment.

## W64F-D05 — Administrative Enforcement Directive
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/policy/fsis-directives/5100.3

NOIE provides prior notice of intended withholding/suspension in applicable cases and generally gives an establishment a response opportunity.

## W64F-D06 — MPI Directory
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/establishments/meat-poultry-and-egg-product-inspection-directory

Weekly establishment data include species slaughtered, size and categorical production information.

## W64F-D07 — Grant of Inspection
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/policy/fsis-directives/5220.1

FSIS procedures define voluntary suspension/withdrawal, withdrawal and reinstatement of federal inspection grants.

## W64F-D08 — Concentration Evidence
USDA research notes past single beef-plant failures representing roughly 6% of U.S. processing capacity and documents material farm-to-wholesale spread effects.

Use as mechanism evidence, not current exposure.
