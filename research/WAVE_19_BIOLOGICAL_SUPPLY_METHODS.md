# Wave 19B — Research Methods

Namespace: **W19B**

## W19B-M01 — Detection/Confirmation/Public Clock
Store:
sample/detection date,
lab confirmation,
agency publication,
first ingest.

Do not backdate confirmation to sample collection.

## W19B-M02 — Commercial-vs-Backyard Split
Backyard detections and commercial-production removals have different economic effects.

Never aggregate bird counts blindly.

## W19B-M03 — Production-Type Capacity Mapping
Translate birds/premises into production capacity using production type and point-in-time industry structure.

## W19B-M04 — Depopulation Timing Model
Confirmed affected birds are not necessarily removed at the exact public confirmation timestamp.

Use policy/actual response data to estimate removal timing.

## W19B-M05 — Control-Area Exposure
Movement controls can affect noninfected premises.

Model geographic/permit friction separately from infected capacity.

## W19B-M06 — Seasonal/Flyway Baseline
HPAI risk is seasonal and migratory.

Use matched season/flyway controls before claiming cluster information.

## W19B-M07 — Herd-State Vintage Ledger
APHIS livestock maps can revise recent/cumulative case counts.

Archive weekday snapshots.

## W19B-M08 — Regulatory-Status Versioning
Federal Orders/guidance change over time.

Movement/testing rules must match the historical event date.

## W19B-M09 — Screwworm Proximity Geometry
Use actual case coordinates/region where available and livestock-route geography.

State-level distance alone is too coarse.

## W19B-M10 — Port Capacity Accounting
Reopened/closed livestock ports differ in normal head throughput and species eligibility.

Weight accordingly.

## W19B-M11 — Import Substitution Control
Blocked Mexican cattle may be partly offset by domestic supply or other imports.

Estimate substitute capacity before inferring price pressure.

## W19B-M12 — Quarantine Polygon Vintage
Archive every quarantine map/boundary version and effective date.

## W19B-M13 — Commercial Acreage Gate
A large quarantine with no commercial agriculture should not be scored as equivalent to one covering productive acreage.

## W19B-M14 — Crop-Season Alignment
Quarantine impact depends on crop stage, harvest timing, host commodity and movement rules.

## W19B-M15 — Eradication Release Model
Quarantine removal follows pest-specific criteria.

Use event-specific recovery, not a generic duration.

## W19B-M16 — Recall Vintage Ledger
FSIS recalls/alerts can expand, revise, retract or terminate.

Each public update is a separate information event.

## W19B-M17 — Pounds-vs-Capacity Distinction
Recalled pounds are finished inventory removed, not necessarily future production capacity.

Keep inventory loss and plant-capacity loss separate.

## W19B-M18 — Ingredient Graph Identity
Map recalled ingredient lots to downstream products/establishments only with source evidence.

## W19B-M19 — Physical-Market Validation
Validate biological shocks first against:
egg/cattle/meat/crop cash prices,
inventories,
slaughter/processing,
imports,
production.

## W19B-M20 — Financial Promotion Gate
Only test futures/equities after showing OOS incremental prediction of a real physical supply outcome.
