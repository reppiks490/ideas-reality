# Wave 9O — Research Methods

Namespace: W9O

## W9O-M01 — Nomination Cycle Reconstruction
Gas nominations and renominations are revisions to an expected schedule. Preserve every cycle and compare sequentially.

## W9O-M02 — Pipeline Location Graph
Normalize proprietary pipeline location codes to geographic/functional nodes without assuming name equality across pipelines.

## W9O-M03 — Capacity Utilization Denominator
Use operating/technical capacity appropriate to each point. Do not compare raw scheduled quantity across heterogeneous points.

## W9O-M04 — Critical-Notice NLP with Physical Fields
Extract affected facility/location, start/end, capacity impact and reason before text sentiment.

## W9O-M05 — Planned-vs-Unplanned Constraint Split
Planned maintenance has different information content from forced/unplanned interruption.

## W9O-M06 — Substitute-Route Capacity
Constraint severity = lost capacity × inability to reroute through adjacent pipelines/storage/LNG.

## W9O-M07 — Gas-Day Clock Normalization
Normalize gas-day boundaries, CET/CEST, U.S. pipeline central clock time and UTC before lead-lag tests.

## W9O-M08 — Nomination-to-Flow Reality Gap
Measure expectation revision and delivery error separately.

## W9O-M09 — Estimated-vs-Confirmed Storage Status
GIE records can carry estimated/confirmed/no-data status. Confidence must reflect it.

## W9O-M10 — Satellite Control-Pixel Design
For night-light events use matched unaffected pixels controlling for moon, clouds, snow, seasonal lights and view geometry.

## W9O-M11 — Recovery Curve from Radiance
Estimate restoration trajectory rather than one binary outage observation.

## W9O-M12 — ABS Vintage Cohorts
Compare loans within origination/vintage/credit-quality cohorts rather than across incomparable pools.

## W9O-M13 — Loan-State Transition Matrix
Model Current -> 30D -> 60D -> 90D -> default/recovery/prepay rather than aggregate delinquency level only.

## W9O-M14 — Trust-to-Originator Mapping
Map ABS trusts/depositors/sponsors to economic originators and public issuers with effective dates/confidence.

## W9O-M15 — Complaint Publication-Lag Model
Model received -> sent-to-company -> public date. No backtest may expose complaint details before public publication.

## W9O-M16 — Complaint Exposure Denominator
Normalize complaint counts by plausible customer/product exposure; raw company counts are misleading.

## W9O-M17 — Prospective Trial Version Store
ClinicalTrials.gov v2 is primarily a current-record API. Archive every observed version and source data timestamp prospectively.

## W9O-M18 — Administrative-vs-Material Trial Diff
Classify edits into administrative, timing, enrollment, endpoint, safety/status and results categories before market testing.

## W9O-M19 — Coverage-to-Revenue Materiality
Map CMS coverage changes to eligible population, procedure/product exposure and reimbursement economics before equity testing.

## W9O-M20 — Regulatory Chain Causality
Validate trial -> FDA -> CMS -> utilization sequentially. Failure of an intermediate link kills the causal story.

## W9O-M21 — Event/Public-Time Dual Clock
For OSHA/operational records keep event_time and first_public_time separately.

## W9O-M22 — Independent Confirmation Score
Reward confirmations from sources with genuinely independent collection mechanisms; do not double-count syndicated/repackaged copies.
