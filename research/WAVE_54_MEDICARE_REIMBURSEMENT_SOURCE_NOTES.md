# Wave 54C — Source Notes

Namespace: **W54C**

## W54C-D01 — Medicare Coverage Database
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/

NCD/NCA tracking sheets provide review status, public comment windows, proposed decision due dates and expected completion dates.

## W54C-D02 — MCD Downloads
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/downloads/downloads.aspx

CMS provides downloadable current/retired LCD, Article and NCD datasets with data-as-of and release dates.

## W54C-D03 — Local Coverage Determination Process
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/cms-guide-medical-technology-companies-and-other-interested-parties/coverage/local-coverage-determination-process-and-timeline

CMS states final LCD publication begins a required notice period of at least 45 days before the LCD becomes effective.

## W54C-D04 — Coverage With Evidence Development
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/evidence

CED allows NCD coverage conditioned on participation in evidence generation and can later be reconsidered as evidence matures.

## W54C-D05 — TCET
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/final-notice-transitional-coverage-emerging-technologies-cms-3421-fn

TCET is designed for certain FDA-designated Breakthrough Devices. CMS anticipates up to five candidates per year and states a goal of finalizing qualifying NCDs within six months after FDA market authorization.

## W54C-D06 — HCPCS Level II
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system

CMS accepts quarterly drug/biological applications and biannual non-drug/non-biological applications.

## W54C-D07 — HCPCS Preliminary / Final Decisions
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system/current-prior-years-level-II-coding-decisions
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system/public-meetings

Public-meeting materials expose preliminary coding, benefit-category and payment determinations; final decisions can differ.

## W54C-D08 — HCPCS Quarterly Update
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/healthcare-common-procedure-system/quarterly-update

CMS publishes complete quarterly code-set files with explicit effective dates.

## W54C-D09 — OPPS Quarterly Addenda
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/hospital-outpatient-pps/quarterly-addenda-updates

Quarterly Addendum A/B files provide status indicators, APC assignments and OPPS payment rates in effect at the beginning of each quarter.

## W54C-D10 — OPPS Pass-Through / New Technology APC
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/hospital-outpatient/pass-through-payment-status-new-technology-ambulatory-payment-classification-apc

CMS evaluates pass-through applications quarterly; approved fast-track applications can become effective at the start of the next quarter.

## W54C-D11 — IPPS NTAP
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/acute-inpatient-pps/new-medical-services-and-new-technologies

Annual IPPS implementation files identify newly approved, continued and discontinued new technology add-on payments.

## W54C-D12 — Medicare Part B ASP Files
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/part-b-drugs/asp-pricing-files

CMS publishes quarterly Part B payment limits and NDC-HCPCS crosswalks, including revised historical quarterly files.

## W54C-D13 — Medicare Part B Drug ASP Methodology
Primary:
https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-for-service-providers/part-b-drugs/average-drug-sales-price

Most separately payable Part B drugs/biologicals use ASP-based payment methodology; exact statutory adjustments/rules must be versioned.

## W54C-D14 — Preliminary Quarterly Drug Spending
Primary:
https://data.cms.gov/summary-statistics-on-use-and-payments/medicare-medicaid-spending-by-drug

CMS now publishes preliminary quarterly Part B/Part D drug utilization/spending data. Starting July 2026, Part B extraction about three months after the reporting quarter captures over 94% of records, with maturity caveats.
