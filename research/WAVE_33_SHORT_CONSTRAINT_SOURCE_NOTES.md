# Wave 33S — Source Notes

Namespace: **W33S**

## W33S-D01 — SEC Fails-to-Deliver
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/data/foiadocsfailsdatahtm

Fields include settlement date, CUSIP, symbol, issuer description, prior-day price and total fails outstanding. Publication is twice monthly with material lag.

## W33S-D02 — FINRA Short Interest
Primary:
https://www.finra.org/filing-reporting/regulatory-filing-systems/short-interest

Firms report aggregate customer/proprietary short positions twice monthly; FINRA publishes a designated settlement/due/publication calendar.

## W33S-D03 — FINRA Daily Short Sale Volume
Primary:
https://www.finra.org/finra-data/browse-catalog/short-sale-volume-data/daily-short-sale-volume-files

Covers short-sale trades executed and reported to FINRA TRFs, ADF or ORF for public dissemination. Includes Short Volume, Short Exempt Volume and Total Volume within that scope.

## W33S-D04 — Nasdaq Reg SHO Threshold List
Primary:
https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/Trader.aspx?id=RegSHOThreshold

Daily list/download with file creation timestamp and current threshold flags for Nasdaq securities.

## W33S-D05 — NYSE Reg SHO Threshold Securities
Primary:
https://www.nyse.com/regulation/threshold-securities

NYSE provides current/date-selectable threshold-security downloads.

## W33S-D06 — SEC Regulation SHO
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/investor/pubs/regsho.htm

Defines Rule 204 close-out obligations, threshold-security criteria and participant-level close-out requirements.

## W33S-D07 — Rule 203 Threshold Definition
Threshold status requires aggregate fails >=10,000 shares and >=0.5% of shares outstanding for five consecutive settlement days; removal requires five consecutive days below threshold.

## W33S-D08 — Listed Options / Rates / Dividends
Use existing licensed/public options data plus point-in-time rates/dividend expectations for synthetic-forward and implied-borrow research.

## W33S-D09 — FINRA SLATE Future Gate
Primary:
https://www.finra.org/filing-reporting/slate

Current FINRA page states implementation has been extended until Sept. 28, 2028. Rule 6540 dissemination design is architecture only until verified live.

## W33S-D10 — Empirical Short-Constraint Literature
Research on lending fees, threshold securities, FTDs and short squeezes supports the mechanism that borrow/settlement constraints can affect price discovery and squeeze risk.
Use as mechanism evidence, not proof of current alpha.
