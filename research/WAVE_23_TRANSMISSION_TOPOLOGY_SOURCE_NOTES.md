# Wave 23T — Source Notes

Namespace: **W23T**

## W23T-D01 — PJM Real-Time Marginal Value
Primary:
https://dataminer2.pjm.com/feed/rt_marginal_value

PJM publishes monitored facility, contingency facility, transmission constraint penalty factor, limit-control percentage and shadow price at five-minute granularity.

## W23T-D02 — PJM Transmission Limits
Primary:
https://dataminer2.pjm.com/feed/transmission_limits

PJM states the current transmission-limits feed is populated when constraints occur and updates every five minutes during the operating day.

## W23T-D03 — PJM Day-Ahead Constraints
Primary:
https://dataminer2.pjm.com/feed/da_transconstraints
https://dataminer2.pjm.com/feed/da_marginal_value

Use for point-in-time expected congestion before real-time.

## W23T-D04 — PJM Transfer Limits & Flows
Primary:
https://dataminer2.pjm.com/feed/transfer_limits_and_flows

Provides actual average transfers and transfer limits for defined areas.

## W23T-D05 — PJM Transmission Outages
Primary documentation:
https://www.pjm.com/-/media/DotCom/etools/edart/edart-tfol-reports.pdf

Public TFOL/OASIS includes current/future/historical non-market-sensitive transmission outages and supports XML download.

## W23T-D06 — ERCOT SCED Binding Constraints
Primary:
https://www.ercot.com/mp/data-products/data-product-details?id=NP6-86-CD

Public CSV/XML/ZIP data include overloaded element, contingency, shadow price, max shadow price, limit, flow/value and violation.

## W23T-D07 — ERCOT Consolidated Transmission Outages
Primary:
https://www.ercot.com/mp/data-products/data-product-details?id=NP3-157-CD

Combines proposed, approved/accepted, withdrawn and rejected transmission outage reports.

## W23T-D08 — CAISO OASIS
Primary API specification:
https://www.caiso.com/Documents/OASISAPISpecification.pdf

CAISO publishes intertie/interface/branch/nomogram shadow prices and transmission outage information.

## W23T-D09 — CAISO Publication Clocks
CAISO tariff/communications documentation specifies day-ahead and real-time publication schedules for outages and binding transmission constraints.

## W23T-D10 — Empirical Transmission Research
Recent U.S. transmission-value research shows a small fraction of high-congestion hours drives a disproportionate share of transmission value and that unforeseen intraday events are important.

Use as mechanism evidence, not proof of alpha.
