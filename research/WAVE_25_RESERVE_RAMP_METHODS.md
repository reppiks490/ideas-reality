# Wave 25R — Research Methods

Namespace: **W25R**

## W25R-M01 — Market-Design Versioning
Version ancillary products and scarcity rules by effective date.

## W25R-M02 — RTC+B Regime Break
ERCOT pre-Dec-5-2025 and post-RTC+B are separate regimes.

## W25R-M03 — Product-Specific Duration
Use actual response/sustainment requirements.

## W25R-M04 — Nominal-vs-Feasible Reserve
Separate qualified/offered/awarded MW from physically feasible reserve.

## W25R-M05 — SOC Availability Mask
Battery MW requires energy/SOC feasibility.

## W25R-M06 — Interval Availability Ledger
Archive each SCED/settlement interval and public timestamp.

## W25R-M07 — DAM-vs-RT Separation
Day-ahead is expectation; real-time deviation is new information.

## W25R-M08 — Demand-Curve Context
Interpret price relative to contemporaneous scarcity curve.

## W25R-M09 — Deliverability Overlay
Join reserve with transmission topology.

## W25R-M10 — Fuel Sufficiency Overlay
Nominal reserve may be fuel-constrained.

## W25R-M11 — Ramp Direction Split
Up/down ramp scarcity are distinct.

## W25R-M12 — Forecast Vintage Alignment
Use only forecasts available before the operating interval.

## W25R-M13 — Common-Cause Control
Weather/fuel can drive both outages and reserve scarcity.

## W25R-M14 — Reserve Migration Accounting
Composition change is not necessarily total reserve change.

## W25R-M15 — Price-Adder Semantics
Version ERCOT adders historically.

## W25R-M16 — Cross-ISO Normalization
Map reserve products by response/duration/market design before comparison.

## W25R-M17 — Scarcity Threshold Pre-Registration
Define thresholds before holdout testing.

## W25R-M18 — Lead-Lag Guard
Source publication must precede claimed early-warning outcome.

## W25R-M19 — Negative Controls
Test reserve spikes without energy spikes and vice versa.

## W25R-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Require OOS prediction of reliability/dispatch/ramp/scarcity outcome.
