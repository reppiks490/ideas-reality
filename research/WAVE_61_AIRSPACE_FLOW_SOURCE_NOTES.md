# Wave 61F — Source Notes

Namespace: **W61F**

## W61F-D01 — FAA National Airspace System Status
Primary:
https://nasstatus.faa.gov/

FAA publishes current/planned airport and en-route traffic-management constraints.

## W61F-D02 — ATCSCC Advisory Database
Primary:
https://www.fly.faa.gov/adv/

Public advisories include ground stops, GDPs, route advisories, FCAs, operations plans and cancellations.

## W61F-D03 — FAA NOTAM
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/pilots/safety/notams_tfr
https://notams.aim.faa.gov/notamSearch/

NOTAMs provide time-critical information including runway/taxiway/ramp restrictions and procedure changes.

## W61F-D04 — NOTAM Management Service
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/about/initiatives/notam

FAA's modernized NOTAM service supports near-real-time operational data exchange; API access terms must be confirmed before implementation.

## W61F-D05 — Airport Construction
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/airports/runway_safety/runway_construction

FAA publishes construction-related runway/taxiway resources and notices.

## W61F-D06 — BTS On-Time Performance
Primary:
https://www.transtats.bts.gov/

Monthly flight-level data include scheduled/actual times, delays, cancellations, diversions and cause fields.

## W61F-D07 — BTS Delay Causes
Primary:
https://www.transtats.bts.gov/OT_Delay/

Use as retrospective attribution validation.

## W61F-D08 — FAA Operations Plan Text
ATCSCC operations plans explicitly list terminal/en-route constraints and staffing triggers.

## W61F-D09 — W48 Boundary
W48 airworthiness governs aircraft-type regulatory permission/maintenance safety.
W61F governs network/airport flow capacity.

## W61F-D10 — Existing Repo Cross-Sources
Fuse with weather, W35 subsea/digital only where relevant, W29L labor, W34 digital infrastructure, fuel/energy and logistics layers.
