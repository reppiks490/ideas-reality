# Wave 22G — Source Notes

Namespace: **W22G**

## W22G-D01 — FERC Order No. 809
Primary:
https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2020-08/M-1.pdf

Order 809 changed the nationwide Timely Nomination Cycle deadline to 1:00 p.m. CCT and incorporated three intraday nomination opportunities at 10:00 a.m., 2:30 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. CCT, while keeping the gas day beginning at 9:00 a.m. CCT.

## W22G-D02 — FERC Pipeline Posting Requirements
Primary:
https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/OrderNo.720.pdf
https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2020-06/order-720-b.pdf

FERC requires major non-interstate pipelines to post daily scheduled-flow and design-capacity information at qualifying receipt/delivery points and regulates interstate-pipeline operational transparency under Part 284.

## W22G-D03 — FERC Part 284 Informational Postings
Primary legal framework:
18 CFR 284.13.

Research exact current requirements for capacity, scheduling, storage, no-notice service, Index of Customers and capacity release.

## W22G-D04 — Williams Transco 1Line
Primary:
https://www.1line.williams.com/

Public critical notices include maintenance, constraints and force-majeure events with post/effective times, affected segments/directions and, in some cases, explicit available quantities / estimated shipper cuts.

## W22G-D05 — Transco Critical-Day / OFO Training
Public Transco material explains Scheduling and Imbalance OFOs, shortage/surplus direction, tolerances, penalties and minimum notice practices.

Use operator tariff/current notice text as final authority for historical state.

## W22G-D06 — Kinder Morgan Informational Postings
Primary:
https://pipeline2.kindermorgan.com/

Pipelines including El Paso Natural Gas, Tennessee Gas Pipeline and Natural Gas Pipeline Company of America publish operational notices, OFOs, force majeure, capacity offerings and related data.

## W22G-D07 — El Paso Natural Gas Linepack Notices
2026 public EPNG operational notices explicitly report system linepack levels, day-over-day drafts, affected locations, imbalance tolerances and required shipper actions.

These are unusually direct physical-network state observations.

## W22G-D08 — Capacity Release / ROFR
Interstate pipeline informational-posting systems publish capacity-release and right-of-first-refusal offerings under FERC tariffs.

Fields can include MDQ, receipt/delivery path, term, bid deadline and tariff-rate context.

## W22G-D09 — FERC No-Notice Service
FERC requires delayed posting of no-notice transportation estimates and later updates.

Use only at actual public availability time.
