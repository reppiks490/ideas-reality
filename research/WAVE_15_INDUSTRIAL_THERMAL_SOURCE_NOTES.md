# Wave 15X — Source Notes

Namespace: **W15X**

## W15X-D01 — TCEQ Air Emission Event Reports
Primary:
https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/emission-events/eventreporting
https://www2.tceq.texas.gov/oce/eer/

TCEQ states initial notification is required within 24 hours of discovery for qualifying emissions events, with final report generally due 14 days after event end. The public database includes reportable emissions, facility identity, event timing and narrative.

## W15X-D02 — South Coast AQMD FENS
Primary:
https://xappp.aqmd.gov/FENS/Public
https://www.aqmd.gov/home/rules-compliance/compliance/r1118/community-notifications

South Coast AQMD states FENS displays received flare-event notifications in real time and that the data are preliminary.

Rule 1118 includes planned and unplanned notification requirements and threshold-based timing.

## W15X-D03 — NASA FIRMS
Primary:
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/

NASA documents global NRT active-fire/thermal-anomaly availability generally within 3 hours, with faster RT/URT service in the U.S./Canada. VIIRS descriptions explicitly note detection of thermal anomalies including gas flares.

## W15X-D04 — FIRMS Missing/Data Availability
Primary:
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/data_availability/
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/missing_data/

Use to prevent false "flare disappeared" signals during missing observations.

## W15X-D05 — Industrial Flare Research
VIIRS Nightfire and related peer-reviewed remote-sensing work supports facility-level monitoring of industrial gas flaring.

Treat research literature as measurement-method evidence, not proof of market alpha.

## W15X-D06 — BSEE Hurricane Activity Updates
Primary:
https://www.bsee.gov/resources-tools/hurricane/hurricane-history

BSEE states its Gulf hurricane response team posts daily evacuation and oil/gas production shut-in statistics during relevant storm events.

## W15X-D07 — USCG National Response Center
Primary:
https://nrc.uscg.mil/

The National Response Center publishes initial public incident spreadsheets and warns they are unvalidated. Real-time incident-notification profiles are restricted to eligible government entities.

## W15X-D08 — PHMSA Incident Data
Primary:
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/data-and-statistics/pipeline/pipeline-incident-20-year-trends

Use for pipeline incident severity/history and confirmation, with actual public-release lag respected.

## W15X-D09 — EPA CAMPD
Primary:
https://campd.epa.gov/
https://www.epa.gov/power-sector/cam-api-portal

EPA exposes detailed hourly power-sector emissions/operating data. EPA guidance states sources submit hourly data within 30 days after each calendar quarter; therefore it is not a short-latency operational trigger.

## W15X-D10 — OSHA Severe Injury Reports
Primary:
https://www.osha.gov/severe-injury-reports

OSHA requires rapid employer reporting of severe injuries, but OSHA's public documentation states the public SIR dataset has about a six-month lag. Use only for historical operational-risk research.
