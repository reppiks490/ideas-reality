# Wave 14I — Source Notes

Namespace: **W14I**

## W14I-D01 — NRC Power Reactor Status Reports
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/event-status/reactor-status/

NRC provides current and historical unit-level daily power status. NRC notes data are collected between 4 a.m. and 8 a.m. Eastern each day. Historical reports can include reason/comment, change indicator and scram/trip count.

## W14I-D02 — NRC Event Notification Reports
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/event-status/event/

Event reports include notification/event timestamps, facility/unit, power/mode, reportability category, narrative and updates. Filter to power-relevant reactor events; the event feed also contains non-power nuclear/material events. The licensee notification timestamp is not automatically the public-web timestamp; archive first public appearance/updates separately.

## W14I-D03 — Bureau of Reclamation RISE
Primary:
https://data.usbr.gov/rise/api
https://data.usbr.gov/time-series

RISE exposes JSON-queryable time-series data. Key reservoir/dam records can include daily storage, elevation, inflow and powerplant/total releases, with item-specific update cadence.

## W14I-D04 — Panama Canal Authority
Primary:
https://pancanal.com/en/maritime-services/vessel-eta-and-transit-booking/
https://pancanal.com/en/maritime-services/

Research Advisory to Shipping archives and operational pages for slot capacity, draft and booking changes.

2026 advisories demonstrate that ACP can alter future daily Panamax/Neopanamax slot counts, draft limits and segment allocation based on water conditions.

## W14I-D05 — Panama Transit Reservation Rules
Primary 2026 rules/advisories are published by ACP under pancanal.com.

Booking periods and slot-allocation mechanisms are rule-defined and may be changed by advisory. Version them historically.

## W14I-D06 — NOAA GLERL Daily Great Lakes Ice
Primary:
https://www.glerl.noaa.gov/data/ice/
https://apps.glerl.noaa.gov/erddap/info/glerlIce/index.html

Daily lake-specific ice concentration is available through NOAA GLERL/ERDDAP and historical archives.

## W14I-D07 — U.S. National Ice Center Great Lakes Data
Primary:
https://usicecenter.gov/Products/GreatLakesData

Daily analysis includes GIS/ASCII/GRIB data with ice concentration/type/floe information.

## W14I-D08 — Great Lakes Operational Context
Pair ice data with:
USACE lock status/navigation,
U.S. Coast Guard icebreaking assistance,
vessel/port flow and commodity inventory.

## W14I-D09 — Negative Evidence Requirement
GAO 2024 analysis found Great Lakes aggregate ice coverage was generally not associated with selected economic indicators, and industry adapts through stockpiling/operations.

This evidence is a reason to test *route-specific effective constraint after mitigation*, not abandon the source or overclaim raw ice cover.
