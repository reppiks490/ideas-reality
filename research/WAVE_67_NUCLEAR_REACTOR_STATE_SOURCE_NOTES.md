# Wave 67N — Source Notes

Namespace: **W67N**

## W67N-D01 — NRC Power Reactor Status Reports
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/event-status/reactor-status/

NRC publishes current/archived operating status for U.S. commercial power reactors with unit power percentage and outage/reduction comments.

## W67N-D02 — Reactor Status Raw Data
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/documents-reports/nrc-data

NRC provides raw reactor-status datasets/data dictionaries and describes the reactor-status dataset as updated daily.

## W67N-D03 — NRC Event Notification Reports
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/documents-reports/document-collections/events-reports-associated-with/event-notification-reports

Reports include event/notification date-time, facility/unit, event text, regulatory reporting basis and reactor state.

## W67N-D04 — 10 CFR 50.72/50.73 Guidance
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/regulations-legislation/nureg-series-publications/publications-prepared-by-nrc-staff/event-reporting-guidelines-10-cfr-5072-and-5073-nureg-1022

Use exact reporting-rule semantics.

## W67N-D05 — NRC Scram Data
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/documents-reports/nrc-data

NRC publishes operating-reactor scram summaries and related dashboard information.

## W67N-D06 — Reactor Oversight Process
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/reactors/operating/oversight.html

Performance indicators and inspection findings provide slower unit-level safety/performance context.

## W67N-D07 — NRC Enforcement
Primary:
https://www.nrc.gov/documents-reports/document-collections

Significant enforcement actions, orders and notices can provide structural operating constraints.

## W67N-D08 — NRC Part 21 Reports
