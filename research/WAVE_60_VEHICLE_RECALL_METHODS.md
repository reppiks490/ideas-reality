# Wave 60V — Research Methods

Namespace: **W60V**

## W60V-M01 — Publication Clock
Store NHTSA publication/open/close/recall dates separately.

## W60V-M02 — Complaint Allegation Status
Consumer complaint is an allegation, not verified defect.

## W60V-M03 — Exposure Denominator
Normalize complaint counts by vehicles in service where estimable.

## W60V-M04 — Duplicate Complaint Control
Deduplicate repeat/substantially identical reports carefully.

## W60V-M05 — Investigation State Versioning
Archive every ODI status transition and document.

## W60V-M06 — Recall Campaign Identity
Use NHTSA campaign number as primary regulatory identifier.

## W60V-M07 — Population Revision
Recall population can be amended; preserve each vintage.

## W60V-M08 — Remedy Versioning
Initial/interim/final remedy instructions can change.

## W60V-M09 — Completion Cohort
Completion rates depend on age, geography, severity and owner contactability.

## W60V-M10 — Supplier Mapping Confidence
Component-supplier attribution requires sourced evidence.

## W60V-M11 — Platform Crosswalk
Maintain exact model/year/plant/platform/part mapping.

## W60V-M12 — Current Production Gate
Do not infer production disruption from legacy-vehicle recall.

## W60V-M13 — Stop-Sale Confirmation
Require official/OEM/dealer evidence.

## W60V-M14 — OTA vs Hardware Economics
Software recalls have different marginal cost and downtime.

## W60V-M15 — Warranty Accounting Clock
Company accruals/earnings disclosures are later truth layers.

## W60V-M16 — Common-News Control
Earnings, labor and macro auto demand can coincide with recall events.

## W60V-M17 — Foreign-to-U.S. Timing
Use first public foreign action time and prove platform/component overlap.

## W60V-M18 — Supplier Spillover Negative Controls
Unrelated suppliers/models should not show same operational effect.

## W60V-M19 — Event Overlap Deduplication
Multiple campaigns may stem from one root defect.

## W60V-M20 — Operational Promotion Gate
Before return testing require OOS improvement in:
recall escalation,
completion,
parts demand,
stop-sale duration,
production loss,
or warranty cost.
