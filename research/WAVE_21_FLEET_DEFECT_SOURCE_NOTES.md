# Wave 21D — Source Notes

Namespace: **W21D**

## W21D-D01 — FAA Emergency Airworthiness Directives
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/continued_operation/ad/type_pub/type_emerg
https://drs.faa.gov/

FAA states an Emergency AD is issued when an unsafe condition requires immediate action. Emergency AD actual notice may precede Federal Register publication.

## W21D-D02 — FAA Dynamic Regulatory System
Primary:
https://drs.faa.gov/

DRS provides searchable AD/EAD documents, issue/effective/applicability and compliance material, with downloadable search results.

## W21D-D03 — FAA Aircraft Registry
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/licenses_certificates/aircraft_certification/aircraft_registry/releasable_aircraft_download

FAA states the downloadable U.S. civil aircraft registry is refreshed daily.

## W21D-D04 — FAA Service Difficulty Reporting System
Primary:
https://sdrs.faa.gov/
https://www.faa.gov/av-info/download_SDR

Public processed SDRs contain aircraft/component defect information and annual CSV downloads.

## W21D-D05 — SDR Reporting Rules
Primary:
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-G/part-121/subpart-V/section-121.703
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-G/part-135/subpart-O/section-135.415
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-H/part-145/subpart-E/section-145.221

Use the eCFR rule text effective at the event date plus FAA SDR guidance. Reporter due time is not public SDR availability; W21D-D06 remains the governing public-latency caveat.

Part 121/135 reports generally cover daily periods and are due to FAA within the next 96 hours; repair stations must report qualifying serious failures within 96 hours.

## W21D-D06 — SDR Availability Caveat
FAA's public query explicitly notes recently submitted SDRs are not available until FAA approves the submission.

Therefore SDR is not assumed real-time.

## W21D-D07 — NHTSA Recalls Data/API
Primary:
https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis
https://api.nhtsa.gov/

NHTSA provides recalls by vehicle/campaign and downloadable recall data.

## W21D-D08 — NHTSA Part 573 Timing
NHTSA states manufacturers that determine a safety defect/noncompliance must notify the agency within five business days.

## W21D-D09 — NHTSA Urgent Advisories
Official Part 573/recall records can identify urgent do-not-drive or park-outside instructions, affected population and remedy schedule.

## W21D-D10 — FRA Safety Actions
Primary:
https://railroads.dot.gov/elibrary

FRA Emergency Orders/Safety Advisories can impose or recommend operating restrictions. Treat mandatory and advisory actions separately and version historically.
