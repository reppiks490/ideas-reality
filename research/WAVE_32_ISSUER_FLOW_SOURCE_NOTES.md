# Wave 32I — Source Notes

Namespace: **W32I**

## W32I-D01 — SEC Item 703 / 2024 Technical Amendments
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2024/34-99778.pdf

The Fifth Circuit vacated the 2023 Share Repurchase Disclosure Modernization rule effective Dec. 19, 2023. SEC technical amendments restored the prior rules, including monthly Item 703 repurchase disclosure in 10-Q/10-K.

## W32I-D02 — SEC Further Announcement on Vacatur
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/whats-new/further-announcement-regarding-share-repurchase-disclosure-modernization-rule

Confirms disclosure requirements reverted to those in effect before the vacated rule.

## W32I-D03 — Rule 10b-18
Primary:
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/subpart-A/subject-group-ECFRbda83517ce4377f/section-240.10b-18

Voluntary safe harbor with manner, timing, price and volume conditions. Normal daily volume condition is 25% of four-week ADTV, with a defined block alternative.

## W32I-D04 — SEC Rule 10b-18 FAQ
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/trading-markets-frequently-asked-questions/division-trading-markets-answers-frequently-asked-questions-concerning-rule-10b-18-safe-harbor

Provides operational interpretation including close timing, block purchases and after-hours conditions.

## W32I-D05 — EDGAR 8-K ASR Agreements
Issuer 8-Ks regularly disclose ASR notional, payment, initial delivery and expected/final settlement terms.

Use exact filing timestamps and contract exhibits.

## W32I-D06 — SEC Rule 415 / 424B5 ATM Prospectus Supplements
ATM prospectus supplements disclose sales agreements and maximum remaining offering capacity.

Capacity is not evidence of actual sale.

## W32I-D07 — Periodic ATM Sales Disclosures
10-Q/10-K and some current filings disclose shares sold and proceeds under ATM programs.

## W32I-D08 — SEC Inline Filing / Company Facts
Use EDGAR filing metadata, statements and share-count/cash-flow tags where semantically appropriate. Do not substitute generic cash-flow repurchase tags for Item 703 monthly execution detail.

## W32I-D09 — Convertible / Capped Call Filings
8-K, 424B5 and exhibit documents disclose convertible principal, conversion terms, capped calls and counterparty arrangements.
