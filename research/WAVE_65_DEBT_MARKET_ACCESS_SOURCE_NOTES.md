# Wave 65D — Source Notes

Namespace: **W65D**

## W65D-D01 — SEC Form 8-K Item 2.03
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/file/form8-kpdf

Item 2.03 requires disclosure of material direct financial obligations, including amount and material payment/acceleration terms.

## W65D-D02 — SEC Form 8-K Item 2.04
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/file/form8-kpdf

Item 2.04 covers material triggering events that accelerate or increase a direct financial obligation and the consequences of the event.

## W65D-D03 — EDGAR Filings
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/

Use filing acceptance timestamps and attached credit agreements/indentures/exhibits.

## W65D-D04 — SEC Prospectus / Pricing Supplements
Forms including 424B2/424B5 and related Rule 433 materials can disclose final or preliminary debt-offering terms.

Exact form depends on offering/registration structure.

## W65D-D05 — Periodic Debt Disclosures
10-K/10-Q debt notes disclose outstanding instruments, maturity schedules, liquidity and covenant information.

Use point-in-time filing vintages.

## W65D-D06 — FINRA TRACE
Primary:
https://www.finra.org/finra-data/fixed-income/about-trade-activity

TRACE disseminates executed corporate-bond trade time, quantity, price and yield and can validate post-issue secondary behavior.

## W65D-D07 — W25C Credit Layer
Reuse W25C transaction-level issuer credit residuals, customer flow and liquidity states.

## W65D-D08 — W32I Issuer Flow
Use W32I when debt financing competes with equity/convertible issuance or supports buybacks.

## W65D-D09 — W51B Restructuring Boundary
W65D ends when ordinary refinancing/default management becomes court-supervised restructuring.

## W65D-D10 — Market Benchmark Data
Use lawful point-in-time Treasury/swap/credit-index data to estimate issuance spread and concession.
