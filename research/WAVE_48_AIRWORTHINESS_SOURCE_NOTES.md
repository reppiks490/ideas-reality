# Wave 48A — Source Notes

Namespace: **W48A**

## W48A-D01 — FAA Service Difficulty Reporting System
Primary:
https://sdrs.faa.gov/
https://av-info.faa.gov/

Public SDR query/download supports fields including difficulty date, registration, make/model, ATA/JASC, part name/make/number, and problem description.

Important limitation:
recent submissions are not public until FAA approves them.

## W48A-D02 — FAA Dynamic Regulatory System
Primary:
https://drs.faa.gov/

DRS publishes/searches ADs, Emergency ADs, NPRMs, TCDS and related regulatory material. Search results can be exported in some DRS views.

## W48A-D03 — FAA Emergency AD Semantics
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/continued_operation/ad/type_pub/type_emerg

FAA states Emergency ADs are issued for unsafe conditions requiring immediate action and are effective to people receiving actual notice; Federal Register follow-up makes the rule effective to all persons.

## W48A-D04 — FAA AD Issuance / Publication
Primary:
https://www.faa.gov/aircraft/air_cert/continued_operation/ad/issue_pub

Federal Register and DRS provide official AD publication/effective information.

## W48A-D05 — FAA SAIB
Primary:
FAA Special Airworthiness Information Bulletins / FAA Order 8110.100 series.

SAIBs contain non-regulatory guidance/recommendations and are non-mandatory.

## W48A-D06 — FAA Aircraft Registry
Primary:
https://registry.faa.gov/

Registry data identify U.S.-registered aircraft by N-number, serial, manufacturer/model, status and related fields. Use point-in-time archives for historical exposure.

## W48A-D07 — FAA Type Certificate Data Sheets
Primary:
FAA DRS TCDS.

Use TCDS for model/type-certificate resolution and configuration context.

## W48A-D08 — Federal Register AD Rules
Primary:
https://www.federalregister.gov/

Final/immediately-adopted AD rules include applicability, effective date, compliance requirements, cost/fleet estimates and incorporated material.

## W48A-D09 — EASA Safety Publications
Primary:
https://ad.easa.europa.eu/

EASA publishes ADs/Emergency ADs and revisions with applicability/effective dates and mandatory actions.

## W48A-D10 — Evidence Boundary
Aircraft accidents/incidents, SDRs, SAIBs and ADs represent different evidence/regulatory states.

Research must preserve that hierarchy before making fleet or financial claims.
