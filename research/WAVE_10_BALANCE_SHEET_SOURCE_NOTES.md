# Wave 10B — Primary Source Notes

Namespace: W10B

## W10B-D01 — New York Fed Primary Dealer Statistics
https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/counterparties/primary-dealers-statistics

Updated Thursdays around 4:15 p.m. ET with prior-week statistics. Data include positions, transactions, financing and fails; automated exports are available.

## W10B-D02 — OFR U.S. Repo Markets Data Release
https://www.financialresearch.gov/short-term-funding-monitor/datasets/repo/

Daily preliminary rates/volumes for centrally cleared and tri-party repo. DVP/GCF generally one-day lag, tri-party roughly two-day lag; final validated series quarterly.

## W10B-D03 — FICC Sponsored Repo Volumes
https://www.financialresearch.gov/hedge-fund-monitor/datasets/ficc/

FICC publishes sponsored repo volumes daily. OFR explains that sponsored repo borrowing is heavily associated with hedge funds and sponsored reverse repo lending with money funds.

## W10B-D04 — Treasury Investor Class Auction Allotments
https://home.treasury.gov/data/investor-class-auction-allotments
https://treasurydirect.gov/help-center/faqs/auction-faqs/

Investor-class data distinguish categories more specifically than indirect/direct bidder labels.

## W10B-D05 — Federal Reserve H.8
https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h8/

Weekly aggregate commercial-bank assets/liabilities with historical revisions and benchmark changes.

## W10B-D06 — Federal Reserve SCOOS
https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/scoos.htm

Quarterly qualitative dealer financing terms, leverage and collateral/credit-condition survey.

## W10B-D07 — OFR Hedge Fund Monitor
https://www.financialresearch.gov/hedge-fund-monitor/

Combines aggregated SEC Form PF and other public datasets; includes leverage, repo borrowing/exposure and sponsored repo series.

## W10B-D08 — SEC Private Fund Statistics
https://www.sec.gov/data-research/statistics-data-visualizations/private-fund-statistics

Aggregated/masked Form PF statistics. Delayed structural information only.
