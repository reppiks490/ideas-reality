# Wave 20E — Research Methods

Namespace: **W20E**

## W20E-M01 — Request/Announcement/Effective Ledger
Store separately:
request time,
agency announcement,
effective start,
effective end,
extension,
termination.

## W20E-M02 — Endogeneity Control
Relief is issued because stress already exists.

Use pre-relief physical state and matched events before attributing price changes to policy.

## W20E-M03 — Constraint-Specific Capacity Estimate
Translate each waiver only into capacity directly affected by the relaxed rule.

## W20E-M04 — Renewal Vintage Discipline
Each renewal is new information. Never collapse consecutive waivers into one hindsight interval.

## W20E-M05 — Geography/Season Normalization
Fuel-spec constraints and demand vary by geography and season.

## W20E-M06 — Inventory/Flow Validation
Validate relief against inventory, production, pipeline, truck, vessel or grid flows.

## W20E-M07 — FMCSA Commodity Scope
Emergency relief applies only to qualifying direct assistance.

Do not treat all truck traffic in affected states as deregulated.

## W20E-M08 — Logistics Bottleneck Decomposition
HOS relief cannot solve terminal, fuel, road, rail or equipment bottlenecks automatically.

## W20E-M09 — 202(c) Resource Ledger
Store every specified/backup resource and later additions with effective timestamps.

## W20E-M10 — Authorized-vs-Dispatched
Legal authorization is not physical generation.

Require dispatch/load evidence.

## W20E-M11 — RTO Request as Separate Event
Applications may contain reserve/load/outage information before DOE issuance.

Preserve public clock.

## W20E-M12 — Environmental-Limit Counterfactual
Estimate whether the unit would actually have been constrained absent the order.

## W20E-M13 — Jones Request Completeness
Only perfected/complete requests enter capacity-deficit research where status can be determined.

## W20E-M14 — Vessel Availability Verification
Use MARAD/official capacity findings where public; do not infer no U.S. vessel solely from requester assertion.

## W20E-M15 — Cargo/Voyage Normalization
Convert waiver cargo to tons/barrels/MMBtu and vessel-days.

## W20E-M16 — Multi-Relief Duplicate Cause
Several agencies may respond to one shock. Treat as correlated evidence, not independent shocks.

## W20E-M17 — Relief Expiry Hazard
Estimate probability of renewal versus normalization using physical state near expiry.

## W20E-M18 — Market Reaction Decomposition
Separate:
severity-confirmation effect
from
capacity-release effect.

They can have opposite signs.

## W20E-M19 — Rare Policy Event Design
Prefer event studies/matched controls and physical intermediate outcomes over high-dimensional models.

## W20E-M20 — Promotion Gate
Require demonstrated effect on usable capacity/backlog/reserves before testing directional financial returns.
