# Wave 19B — Source Notes

Namespace: **W19B**

## W19B-D01 — APHIS HPAI Commercial/Backyard Flocks
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/commercial-backyard-flocks

APHIS publishes current confirmed HPAI detections and production-type context.

## W19B-D02 — APHIS HPAI Response / Depopulation Policy
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/animal-emergencies/hpai

APHIS policy describes quarantine/movement control and rapid stamping-out of infected poultry, with a depopulation goal generally within 24–48 hours or less.

## W19B-D03 — APHIS HPAI Livestock
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/hpai-confirmed-cases-livestock

APHIS states its livestock map is updated each weekday and reports recent/cumulative confirmed livestock cases by state.

## W19B-D04 — Dairy Cattle Movement Requirements
Primary:
APHIS HPAI livestock Federal Order/guidance pages.

Requirements can vary with state disease/surveillance status. Archive every historical version before backtesting.

## W19B-D05 — APHIS New World Screwworm
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/animals/animal-health/livestock-and-poultry-disease/stop-screwworm/current-status

APHIS publishes current geographic status and trade measures. In 2026 it used phased reopening of southern livestock import ports conditional on risk/control milestones.

## W19B-D06 — APHIS Plant Pest / Fruit Fly Quarantines
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-pests-diseases/fruit-flies/fruit-fly-quarantine-maps-descriptions
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/plant-pests-diseases/fruit-flies/fruit-fly-federal-orders-spro-letters

Orders can establish/expand/remove quarantines and may specify commercial acreage plus regulated-movement restrictions.

## W19B-D07 — APHIS Citrus Disease Programs
Primary:
APHIS citrus greening/canker program updates and Federal Orders.

Use effective date, quarantine geometry and commercial acreage from the original notice.

## W19B-D08 — USDA AMS Egg / Poultry Market Data
Primary:
https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news

Useful validation includes daily shell egg prices, weekly shell egg inventory, breaking stock and egg-product processing reports.

## W19B-D09 — USDA FSIS Recall API
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/science-data/developer-resources/recall-api

FSIS states the API provides real-time access to recall/public-health alert data in JSON.

## W19B-D10 — FSIS Recall Rules / Updates
Primary:
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls

Official releases contain establishment, reason, product, pounds/distribution and update/revision information. Establishments must notify FSIS rapidly when certain adulterated/misbranded product enters commerce.
