# Wave 44I — Source Notes

Namespace: **W44I**

## W44I-D01 — USITC Section 337 Overview
Primary:
https://www.usitc.gov/press_room/us337.htm

USITC describes investigation, ALJ initial determination, Commission review and exclusion/CDO remedies.

## W44I-D02 — 45-Day Target-Date Rule
USITC states it must establish a target date within 45 days after institution.

## W44I-D03 — EDIS
Primary:
https://edis.usitc.gov/

USITC electronic docket contains public investigation documents and metadata. Public-content access controls apply.

## W44I-D04 — Remedial Orders
USITC can issue limited/general exclusion orders and cease-and-desist orders when statutory requirements are met.

## W44I-D05 — Presidential Review
USITC states remedial orders are effective when issued and become final after 60 days unless disapproved for policy reasons through presidential/USTR review.

## W44I-D06 — Bond During Review
Commission notices state subject articles may enter during the presidential review period under bond in the amount set by the Commission.

## W44I-D07 — Settlement / Consent Orders
Primary:
USITC Rule 210.21 / Section 337 FAQ.

Investigations can terminate in whole/part based on settlement, license, arbitration or consent order.

## W44I-D08 — Enforcement / Modification
USITC publishes enforcement, modification and rescission proceedings/orders.

## W44I-D09 — Federal Circuit
Final USITC determinations can be appealed to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit.

## W44I-D10 — CBP Boundary
CBP enforces exclusion at the border. Use only public CBP rulings/guidance where available; do not infer confidential shipment-level enforcement.
