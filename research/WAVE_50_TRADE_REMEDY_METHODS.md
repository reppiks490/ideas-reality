# Wave 50T — Research Methods

Namespace: **W50T**

## W50T-M01 — Announcement vs Legal Effect
Store:
press announcement,
Federal Register publication,
effective entry date/time,
CBP implementation notice,
first ingest.

## W50T-M02 — Scope Text Dominates HTS Hint
HTS codes may be provided for convenience; legal merchandise scope controls.

## W50T-M03 — Producer/Exporter Combination Logic
Apply company-specific rates only to valid producer/exporter combinations.

## W50T-M04 — AD/CVD Rate Versioning
Preserve preliminary, final, order, review and amended rates separately.

## W50T-M05 — Cash Deposit != Final Duty
Cash deposit is provisional security, not necessarily final assessed liability.

## W50T-M06 — Critical-Circumstances Gate
Retroactive exposure is applied only where official findings/orders authorize it.

## W50T-M07 — ITC Injury State
Do not treat Commerce dumping/subsidy finding alone as final order where affirmative injury is legally required.

## W50T-M08 — Scope/Circumvention Vintage
Archive every scope/circumvention ruling with effective/applicability details.

## W50T-M09 — HTS Revision Archive
Use the HTS version effective for the entry date.

## W50T-M10 — Chapter 99 Multi-Line Logic
Resolve ordinary classification and special additional-duty provisions together.

## W50T-M11 — Exclusion Versioning
Track exclusion identifier, product description, HTS, start/end and retroactivity.

## W50T-M12 — Origin Rule Evidence
Country of shipment is not always country of origin.

## W50T-M13 — Import Exposure Vintage
Use point-in-time bill-of-lading/customs/company sourcing where legally available.

## W50T-M14 — Value vs Quantity
Tariff cost depends on customs value; physical substitution depends on quantity/capacity.

## W50T-M15 — Front-Load Causality Control
Seasonality/holidays/freight shocks can mimic tariff front-loading.

## W50T-M16 — Supplier Pass-Through Uncertainty
Indirect supplier tariff burden is not automatically fully passed through.

## W50T-M17 — Domestic Protection Capacity Gate
Potential beneficiary must have compatible product and spare capacity.

## W50T-M18 — Legal Change Diff
Diff official rule/HTS text, then validate material changes manually.

## W50T-M19 — Common Trade-Policy Control
Separate broad risk-off tariff headlines from product/company-specific legal exposure.

## W50T-M20 — Intermediate Promotion Gate
Before return testing require OOS improvement in:
landed cost,
import timing,
volume,
supplier price,
inventory,
or margin.
