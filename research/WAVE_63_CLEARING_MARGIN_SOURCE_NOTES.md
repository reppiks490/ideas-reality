## W63M-D03 — ICE Clear U.S. Risk Model
Primary:
https://www.ice.com/clear-us/risk-management

ICE publishes current and historical margin parameter files. Parameter changes become effective in margin calculations on their effective date and are called the following business day.

## W63M-D04 — CFTC Cleared Margin Reports
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/index.htm

Summary information is derived from daily initial-margin data filed by covered DCOs and is published monthly, generally within 10 business days after month-end.

## W63M-D05 — CFTC FCM Financial Data
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/financialfcmdata/index.htm

Selected monthly FCM data include adjusted net capital and customer segregation/funds measures.

## W63M-D06 — CFTC Regulation 1.17
Current CFTC rules tie an FCM minimum adjusted-net-capital requirement, among other floors, to 8% of aggregate risk margin carried for customer/noncustomer positions.

## W63M-D07 — ICE Clear U.S. Financial Resources
Primary:
https://www.ice.com/clear-us/regulation

ICE publicly discloses current initial margin on deposit and components of its default-resource package.

## W63M-D08 — Margin Procyclicality Evidence
2024 Journal of Futures Markets research finds volatility and position exits interact and that stronger anti-procyclical margin design can reduce exits for a given price move.

Use as mechanism evidence only.

## W63M-D09 — Variation Margin Evidence
Empirical research documents procyclical variation-margin liquidity demands during price moves.

Use for scenario design, not participant-level factual claims.
