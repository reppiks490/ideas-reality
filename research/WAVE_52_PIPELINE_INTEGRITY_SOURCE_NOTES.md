# Wave 52P — Source Notes

Namespace: **W52P**

## W52P-D01 — PHMSA Corrective Action Orders
Primary:
https://primis.phmsa.dot.gov/enforcement-data/cases/CAO

PHMSA publishes CAO cases beginning in 2002. CAOs specify corrective actions required for conditions PHMSA considers hazardous to people, property or the environment.

## W52P-D02 — PHMSA CAO Case Documents
Case pages provide public order/amendment documents and, when tied to a reportable event, linked 30-day incident reports.

## W52P-D03 — 2026 Order Mechanics
Current 2026 CAOs provide concrete examples of:
segment shutdown,
regulator-approved restart plan,
incremental restart,
pressure restrictions,
root-cause testing,
remedial work plans.

Use each order's actual terms rather than assuming a standard template.

## W52P-D04 — CAO Closure
Primary:
https://primis.phmsa.dot.gov/enforcement-data/cases/closed/CAO

PHMSA states cases are closed after required corrective actions are satisfactorily completed and the facility is no longer hazardous.

## W52P-D05 — PHMSA Safety Orders
Primary:
PHMSA Pipeline Enforcement Actions / Orders pages.

Safety Orders are a distinct enforcement tool requiring corrective/remedial actions for identified integrity risk.

## W52P-D06 — PHMSA Incident Data
Primary:
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/data-and-statistics/pipeline/pipeline-incident-20-year-trends
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/data-and-statistics/pipeline/pipeline-incident-flagged-files

PHMSA provides raw/flagged incident datasets across pipeline system types.

## W52P-D07 — 30-Day Incident Reports
Primary:
https://primis.phmsa.dot.gov/enforcement-data/incident-report

Federal rules require detailed written reports for reportable pipeline incidents/accidents within 30 days.

## W52P-D08 — Pipeline Performance / Mileage
Primary:
https://www.phmsa.dot.gov/data-and-statistics/pipeline/national-pipeline-performance-measures

PHMSA publishes annual mileage/facility data and operator safety metrics.

## W52P-D09 — National Pipeline Mapping System
Primary:
https://www.npms.phmsa.dot.gov/

NPMS covers PHMSA-jurisdictional gas transmission and hazardous-liquid pipelines, LNG plants and breakout tanks; public geographic detail is restricted and distribution/gathering pipelines are not comprehensively included.
