# Wave 68R — Research Methods

Namespace: **W68R**

## W68R-M01 — Rule-State Vintage
Archive proposal, supplement, final rule, correction, judicial/remand and later amendment separately.

## W68R-M02 — Compliance-Year Identity
Never mix RIN vintage, compliance year and public-data month.

## W68R-M03 — Proposal vs Final
A proposed RVO/reallocation is expectation information, not binding obligation.

## W68R-M04 — SRE Confidentiality
EPA may withhold small-refinery identities.
Do not entity-resolve confidential petitioners by speculation.

## W68R-M05 — Full vs Partial Exemption
Translate partial relief only using EPA-published fraction/volume.

## W68R-M06 — Reallocation Versioning
Projected, proposed and final reallocation are distinct states.

## W68R-M07 — Compliance Deadline Versioning
Archive each extension with publication/effective date.

## W68R-M08 — EMTS Monthly Availability
Use actual EPA posting/vintage time for monthly generated/separated/retired/available RIN data.

## W68R-M09 — Data Revision Ledger
EPA notes aggregated RFS data can change due to resubmissions/remedial actions.
Never overwrite historical public vintages.

## W68R-M10 — RIN Assignment State
Assigned, separated, locked and unlocked are distinct.

## W68R-M11 — Retirement Reason
Compliance retirement differs from invalidation/export/spill/other retirement.

## W68R-M12 — D-Code Nesting
Apply exact eligibility hierarchy for the historical rule state.

## W68R-M13 — D7 Dual-Use Guard
D7 can satisfy cellulosic or BBD obligations under EPA rules but a RIN cannot satisfy both.

## W68R-M14 — Equivalence-Value Fidelity
Physical gallon != one RIN for every pathway.
Use pathway-specific equivalence value.

## W68R-M15 — Feedstock Mix Uncertainty
Incremental renewable diesel/biodiesel production can switch among approved oils/fats/greases.
Use scenario weights.

## W68R-M16 — Import/Export Accounting
Imported renewable fuel can generate/bring eligible RIN supply under rules; renewable-fuel exports create retirement obligations.
Model both.

## W68R-M17 — Physical Production Lag
EPA RIN generation timing can differ from EIA/USDA production publication timing.

## W68R-M18 — RIN Price Rights
Market RIN prices may require licensed sources.
Do not scrape/reproduce restricted feeds.

## W68R-M19 — Emergency Fuel-Waiver Boundary
W20E owns fuel-spec/RVP emergency waiver mechanics.
Do not duplicate them in W68R.

## W68R-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Before financial alpha require OOS improvement in:
RIN deficit/bank,
biofuel production,
feedstock demand,
refinery compliance burden,
or realized retirement/compliance.
