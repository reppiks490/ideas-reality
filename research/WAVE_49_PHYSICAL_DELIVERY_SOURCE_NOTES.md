# Wave 49P — Source Notes

Namespace: **W49P**

## W49P-D01 — CME/COMEX Depository Stocks
Primary:
CME Group metals depository/stock reporting resources.

Approved depositories report exchange-grade inventory daily, including registered and eligible metal and receipts/shipments.

## W49P-D02 — Registered vs Eligible Definition
CME regulatory filings define:
eligible = delivery-spec metal without warrant;
registered = eligible metal with warrant issued.

## W49P-D03 — CME Gold Delivery / Inventory Guidance
Primary:
https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/metals/precious/faq-gold-enhanced-delivery-futures.html

CME states COMEX gold depositories provide daily inventory split between Registered and Eligible and that daily/cumulative delivery notices are available.

## W49P-D10 — Theory of Storage Evidence

Use as mechanism evidence only; independently validate exchange-specific delivery-state features.
