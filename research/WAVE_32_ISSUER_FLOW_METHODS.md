# Wave 32I — Research Methods

Namespace: **W32I**

## W32I-M01 — Authorization != Execution
Never convert announced buyback authorization into actual demand.

## W32I-M02 — Offering Capacity != Issuance
Shelf/ATM registration is only permitted capacity until actual sales are disclosed.

## W32I-M03 — Current SEC Rule Version
The 2023 repurchase modernization rule was vacated. Use restored pre-rule Item 703 monthly disclosure in 2026.

## W32I-M04 — Item 408(d) Exclusion
Issuer-specific 10b5-1 adoption/termination disclosure created by the vacated repurchase rule was removed. Do not assume it exists.

## W32I-M05 — Officer/Director vs Issuer 10b5-1
Item 408(a) officer/director arrangements are distinct from issuer repurchase plans.

## W32I-M06 — 10b-18 Is Voluntary
Safe-harbor limits define a legal scenario envelope, not mandatory execution behavior.

## W32I-M07 — Daily Safe-Harbor Reconstruction
If estimating Rule 10b-18 capacity, use correct four-week ADTV, public float, timing, price and broker/block rules.

## W32I-M08 — Periodic Filing Lag
Item 703 execution becomes public only at 10-Q/10-K filing time. Do not backdate.

## W32I-M09 — ASR Contract Parsing
Extract notional, prepayment, initial delivery, averaging period, discount, settlement and early-termination terms.

## W32I-M10 — ASR Hedging Uncertainty
Dealer acquisition path is not directly observable from issuer contract. Use scenarios/distributions.

## W32I-M11 — Share Delivery != Market Purchase Time
Initial shares delivered under ASR may be borrowed/sourced before dealer completes market purchases.

## W32I-M12 — ATM Realized Sales Clock
Use actual filing-public time for realized ATM shares/proceeds.

## W32I-M13 — Primary vs Secondary Shares
Secondary holder sales do not increase issuer share count; primary issuance does.

## W32I-M14 — Forward-Sale Separation
Forward ATM structures can delay actual issuer share delivery/settlement.

## W32I-M15 — Convertible Hedge Uncertainty
Do not infer exact dealer delta hedge without contract/option terms and market data.

## W32I-M16 — Capped-Call Netting
Estimate capped-call hedge offset separately from convertible dealer hedging.

## W32I-M17 — Compensation Issuance Separation
Employee share issuance is recurring dilution and should not be merged with financing issuance.

## W32I-M18 — Split / Merger Share Reconciliation
Normalize all share counts for splits, mergers, spin-offs and tender retirements.

## W32I-M19 — No Universal Buyback Blackout
Do not hard-code folklore blackout calendars. Use disclosed company policy/plan facts only where sourced.

## W32I-M20 — Mechanical Promotion Gate
Before return alpha require OOS prediction/reconstruction of actual share-count change, dealer/issuer flow, volume share, liquidity impact or index-level issuer-flow state.
