# Wave 58A — Source Notes

Namespace: **W58A**

## W58A-D01 — APHIS HPAI Commercial/Backyard Flocks
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/commercial-backyard-flocks

APHIS maintains a current dashboard of confirmed HPAI detections in commercial and backyard flocks.

## W58A-D02 — APHIS HPAI Livestock
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/hpai-confirmed-cases-livestock

APHIS public livestock case maps/tables provide current HPAI dairy/livestock status.

## W58A-D03 — National Milk Testing Strategy
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/livestock/nmts

NMTS defines affected/unaffected status and national/state bulk-milk surveillance stages.

## W58A-D04 — NASS Chickens and Eggs
Primary:
https://www.nass.usda.gov/Surveys/Guide_to_NASS_Surveys/Chickens_and_Eggs/

Monthly report includes layer inventory, egg production, pullets added and related replacement measures.

## W58A-D05 — USDA AMS Market News
Primary:
https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news

AMS publishes current poultry, egg, livestock and grain price/movement reports at daily/weekly/monthly frequencies depending on product.

## W58A-D06 — USDA ERS HPAI Evidence
USDA ERS has documented large layer depopulation during the 2024–25 HPAI wave and associated egg supply/price effects.

Use as mechanism evidence.

## W58A-D07 — WOAH WAHIS
Primary:
https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/animal-health-and-welfare/disease-data-collection/

Official public animal-disease notifications include geography, species, control measures and follow-up event information.

## W58A-D08 — New World Screwworm Status
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/animals/animal-health/livestock-and-poultry-disease/stop-screwworm/current-status/confirmed

APHIS publishes confirmed animal/wild-fly detections and current mitigation status.

## W58A-D09 — Southern Livestock Import Protocols
Primary:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/live-animal-import/cattle-bison-germplasm/mexico

APHIS publishes port/protocol restrictions for Mexican livestock trade in response to NWS risk.

## W58A-D10 — AMS International Livestock Flows
Primary:
https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news/livestock-poultry-and-grain-international-reports

Weekly U.S.–Mexico/Canada livestock flow reports can validate border-restriction effects.
