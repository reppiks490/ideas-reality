# Wave 58A — Research Methods

Namespace: **W58A**

## W58A-M01 — Confirmation Availability Clock
Store collection date, confirmation/detection date, first public posting and later revisions separately.

## W58A-M02 — Commercial vs Backyard Split
Backyard detections do not receive commercial-capacity weight.

## W58A-M03 — Bird Count vs Productive Capacity
Species/production class determines economic capacity.

Layer hens, broilers, breeders, pullets and turkeys are not interchangeable.

## W58A-M04 — Depopulation Confirmation
Do not assume entire stated bird count is immediately productive capacity lost unless source semantics support it.

## W58A-M05 — Biological Maturation Model
Replacement lag must reflect hatch-to-productive-age biology by production class.

## W58A-M06 — NASS Monthly Availability
Monthly production/inventory is validation and denominator data at actual publication time.

## W58A-M07 — Hatchery Pipeline Vintage
Use historical point-in-time hatchery/pullet estimates available before target date.

## W58A-M08 — Seasonality Control
HPAI is seasonal and poultry demand is strongly seasonal.

## W58A-M09 — Feed-Cost Control
Corn/soymeal and other input-cost changes can move poultry/egg prices simultaneously.

## W58A-M10 — Market-News Product Match
Match AMS price series by exact product/grade/region.

## W58A-M11 — Regional Production Weighting
Weight outbreaks by actual production exposure, not geography alone.

## W58A-M12 — Disease vs Response
Separate biological infection from policy response:
quarantine,
depopulation,
movement restriction,
trade ban.

## W58A-M13 — Dairy Herd Count Semantics
Confirmed herd count is not equivalent to infected cow count or milk lost.

## W58A-M14 — NMTS State-Status Versioning
Archive each affected/unaffected/provisional status vintage.

## W58A-M15 — Port Closure Effective Time
For livestock imports, store announcement, effective closure/reopening and protocol change separately.

## W58A-M16 — Trade Restriction Versioning
Foreign sanitary restrictions can be national, regional, commodity-specific and temporary.

## W58A-M17 — WOAH Notification Vintage
Use first public WAHIS notification/follow-up time.

## W58A-M18 — Import/Export Flow Validation
Validate expected trade shock against AMS/FAS/Customs data at their actual publication latency.

## W58A-M19 — Replacement Censoring
A flock/herd not yet observed as restored remains right-censored.

## W58A-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Before financial alpha require OOS improvement in:
productive inventory,
output,
trade flow,
wholesale price,
or recovery time.
