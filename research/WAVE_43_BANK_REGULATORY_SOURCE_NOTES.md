# Wave 43B — Source Notes

Namespace: **W43B**

## W43B-D01 — FDIC Enforcement Decisions & Orders
Primary:
https://orders.fdic.gov/
https://www.fdic.gov/bank/individual/enforcement/actions.html

FDIC says ED&O contains formal final enforcement actions/notices and is updated monthly with the preceding month's actions.

## W43B-D02 — OCC Enforcement Actions
Primary:
https://apps.occ.gov/EASearch/

Search supports bank/action type, start date, termination date, subject matter and other fields.

## W43B-D03 — Federal Reserve Enforcement Actions
Primary:
https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/enforcement-actions.htm
https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases.htm

Fed releases publicly announce written agreements, C&D orders, PCA directives and terminations.

## W43B-D04 — FDIC Prompt Corrective Action
Primary:
FDIC Section 38 / enforcement manual / 12 CFR Part 324 Subpart H.

General non-CBLR thresholds include:
well capitalized Total RBC >=10%, Tier 1 RBC >=8%, CET1 >=6.5%, leverage >=5%, plus applicable legal conditions.

## W43B-D05 — PCA Consequences
FDIC guidance describes capital-distribution limits, capital-restoration plans, asset-growth restrictions and approval requirements as capital categories deteriorate.

## W43B-D06 — Brokered Deposits
Primary:
https://www.fdic.gov/banker-resource-center/brokered-deposits

Section 29 restricts less-than-well-capitalized institutions; adequately capitalized institutions may seek a waiver under applicable FDIC rules.

## W43B-D07 — FFIEC Call Reports
Primary:
https://cdr.ffiec.gov/public/

Public quarterly Reports of Condition and Income and UBPR data; bulk files and web services are available.

## W43B-D08 — FDIC Bank Data
Primary:
https://banks.data.fdic.gov/

Use public institution/financial histories as a convenient regulatory-data layer, with Call Reports/official filings controlling semantics.

## W43B-D09 — Public Order Examples
Public 2026 FDIC/Fed actions include PCA directives and written agreements; OCC precedent shows explicit asset caps, business restrictions and capital-distribution conditions.

Examples motivate states only; production parsers must read each current order.

## W43B-D10 — Confidentiality Boundary
Examination reports, CAMELS and many supervisory communications are nonpublic.

W43B uses only public formal actions and public financial filings.
