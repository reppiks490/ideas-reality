## W41M-D03 — HSR Confidentiality
Primary:
FTC Formal Interpretation No. 10 / 15 U.S.C. 18a(h).

HSR submissions are exempt from FOIA disclosure and generally confidential except as relevant in proceedings.

## W41M-D04 — 2026 HSR Form State
Primary:
FTC 2026 HSR Forms/Guidance and March 2026 RFI.

The Feb. 10, 2025 updated HSR form was vacated Feb. 12, 2026; FTC appealed; stay was denied Mar. 19, 2026; agencies reverted to the prior form/instructions while allowing voluntary use of the updated form.

## W41M-D05 — SEC Tender Offer Rules
Primary:
SEC Regulation 14E / Tender Offer Rules and Schedules interpretations.

Default tender offers generally remain open at least 20 business days; changes in price/percentage generally trigger 10-business-day extension requirements under Rule 14e-1.

## W41M-D06 — April 16, 2026 Tender Exemptive Order
Primary:
SEC Division of Corporation Finance exemptive order dated Apr. 16, 2026.

Qualifying equity tender offers may use a 10-business-day minimum if the order's conditions are met. Apply prospectively from the order and verify eligibility.
