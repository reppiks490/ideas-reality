# Wave 47S — Source Notes

Namespace: **W47S**

## W47S-D01 — OFAC Sanctions List Service
Primary:
https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-list-service

OFAC SLS is the primary delivery system for current SDN and Consolidated Non-SDN list files and search.

## W47S-D02 — OFAC Machine-Readable Formats
Primary:
https://ofac.treasury.gov/specially-designated-nationals-list-data-formats-data-schemas

OFAC provides XML/CSV and enhanced/advanced schemas for sanctions data and updates them with live list changes.

## W47S-D03 — OFAC 50 Percent Rule
Primary:
https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/399
and related OFAC 50 Percent Rule guidance.

Blocked ownership is aggregated across blocked persons; >=50% aggregate ownership can block an unlisted entity.

## W47S-D04 — OFAC Recent Actions / General Licenses
Primary:
https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions

Use exact General License text for scope and expiration/wind-down clocks.

## W47S-D05 — U.S. Consolidated Screening List
Primary:
https://www.trade.gov/consolidated-screening-list

CSL provides machine-readable/API access consolidating multiple Commerce, State and Treasury restricted-party lists.

## W47S-D06 — BIS Entity List
Primary:
https://www.bis.gov/
EAR supplement no. 4 to part 744.

BIS provides Entity List data and list-specific license requirements/policies; official rule changes are published in the Federal Register.

## W47S-D07 — BIS Denied Persons List
Primary:
BIS Denied Persons List.

Current BIS pages expose effective/expiration dates, Federal Register citations and recent changes.

## W47S-D08 — BIS Temporary Denial Orders
Primary:
15 CFR 766.24 / BIS orders.

A TDO may be issued for up to 180 days, with renewal procedures under the EAR.

## W47S-D09 — BIS Unverified List
Primary:
EAR supplement no. 6 to part 744 / BIS guidance.

UVL status changes transaction requirements and restricts use of license exceptions.

## W47S-D10 — Evidence Boundary
Recent empirical research documents supply-chain and stock-volatility effects around export controls.

Use only as mechanism evidence. Current ICARUS research must independently reconstruct public timing, legal scope and actual supply-chain exposure.
