# Wave 62C — Source Notes

Namespace: **W62C**

## W62C-D01 — CFTC Commitments of Traders
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm

COT provides weekly aggregate large-trader positioning for covered futures markets.

## W62C-D02 — Release Schedule
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/ReleaseSchedule/index.htm

Reports are generally released Friday 3:30 p.m. ET using prior Tuesday positions, subject to holiday delays.

## W62C-D03 — TFF

## W62C-D04 — Disaggregated COT
Primary categories:

## W62C-D05 — Concentration Ratios
Long-format COT includes largest-four and largest-eight reportable trader shares on gross and net bases.

## W62C-D06 — CFTC Public Reporting API
Primary:
https://publicreporting.cftc.gov/

CFTC provides downloadable/API COT datasets.

## W62C-D07 — Historical Compressed
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/HistoricalCompressed/index.htm

Annual historical files support vintage reconstruction and long samples.

## W62C-D08 — Bank Participation Report
Primary:
https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/BankParticipationReports/index.htm

Monthly aggregate U.S./non-U.S. bank long/short futures and gross options positions for markets meeting confidentiality requirements.

## W62C-D09 — BPR Release Semantics
Current CFTC schedule uses first-Tuesday positions and publishes on the first Friday after 3:30 p.m. ET, subject to calendar rules.
