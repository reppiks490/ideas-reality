# Wave 31F — Research Methods

Namespace: **W31F**

## W31F-M01 — Estimated-vs-Actual Separation
Daily slaughter estimates and later actual slaughter are separate series.

## W31F-M02 — Publication Clock
Store report publication time, report date, revision marker and first ingest.

## W31F-M03 — Weekday/Holiday Baseline
Expected slaughter must account for weekday and holiday schedules.

## W31F-M04 — Scheduled Hog Delivery Semantics
Scheduled deliveries are planned flow, not guaranteed slaughter.

## W31F-M05 — Plant Capacity Uncertainty
Maintain lower/base/upper capacity ranges when public capacity is estimated/categorical.

## W31F-M06 — FSIS Public-Latency Gate
Quarterly enforcement tables are delayed; use as historical validation unless faster public notice is archived.

## W31F-M07 — Suspension Scope
Encode whether all inspection or only specific process/product lines are affected.

## W31F-M08 — MPI Weekly Vintage
FSIS current files replace prior editions; archive every weekly version.

## W31F-M09 — Active Directory != Actual Production
Active establishment does not imply full-capacity or daily slaughter.

## W31F-M10 — Ownership Versioning
Packer ownership is point-in-time.

## W31F-M11 — Disease Detection Public Time
Use APHIS first-public confirmation, not internal sample time unless public.

## W31F-M12 — HPAI Premises Deduplication
Multiple updates to one outbreak are one parent incident.

## W31F-M13 — Birds Affected vs Lost Output
Map flock count through production type and replacement cycle.

## W31F-M14 — Control-Area Indirect Effects
Separate movement restriction from depopulation.

## W31F-M15 — Border Policy Clock
Store announcement, effective time and port-specific import rules.

## W31F-M16 — Import Volume Validation
Confirm policy effect with actual crossing/import data where public.

## W31F-M17 — Feedlot Lag
Mexican feeder-cattle shocks reach fed slaughter after biological/time-on-feed lag.

## W31F-M18 — Cold Storage Release Mask
Monthly inventories become available only at public release.

## W31F-M19 — Supply-Side Direction Test
Pre-register signs separately for live animals, wholesale meat, packer margin and substitutes.

## W31F-M20 — Physical Promotion Gate
Before market alpha require OOS prediction of slaughter, delivery backlog, meat output, animal backlog, import flow or storage draw.
