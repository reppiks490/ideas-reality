# Wave 31F — Source Notes

Namespace: **W31F**

## W31F-D01 — USDA AMS Daily Livestock and Poultry Slaughter
https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_3208.pdf
Current-day estimated federally inspected slaughter with week-to-date/prior-week/prior-year comparisons; estimates can be revised.

## W31F-D02 — USDA Actual Slaughter Under Federal Inspection
https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_3658.pdf
Later actual daily slaughter by species/class plus weights and meat production. Validation only for earlier estimates.

## W31F-D03 — USDA Daily Direct Hog Prior Day
https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/ams_2511.pdf
Includes reported slaughter information and future head counts scheduled for delivery to packing plants.

## W31F-D04 — USDA Livestock Mandatory Reporting
https://mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov/
Daily cattle/hog and wholesale-meat reports support purchase, weight and conversion-spread research.

## W31F-D05 — FSIS MPI Directory
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/establishments/meat-poultry-and-egg-product-inspection-directory
Weekly active/inactive establishment data, species/activity/demographic fields, CSV/API.

## W31F-D06 — FSIS Enforcement Semantics
9 CFR Part 500 / FSIS Inspection Methods.
Suspension interrupts FSIS inspection assignment to all or part of an establishment; affected federally inspected production cannot proceed without required inspection.

## W31F-D07 — FSIS Quarterly Enforcement Reports
https://www.fsis.usda.gov/inspection/regulatory-enforcement/quarterly-enforcement-reports/quarterly-enforcement-reports
Tables identify NOIE, withholding, suspension, abeyance and closure by establishment/basis, but are delayed.

## W31F-D08 — APHIS HPAI
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/commercial-backyard-flocks
Confirmed detections plus quarantine/movement-control/depopulation response framework.

## W31F-D09 — APHIS New World Screwworm
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/animals/animal-health/livestock-and-poultry-disease/stop-screwworm/current-status
Current cattle-import port closure/reopening/protocol status. 2026 includes phased southern-port reopening.

## W31F-D10 — USDA NASS Cold Storage
Monthly frozen meat/poultry stocks provide slower inventory-buffer validation.
