# Wave 18G — Source Notes

Namespace: **W18G**

## W18G-D01 — USGS Real-Time Earthquake Feeds
Primary:
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/

USGS provides real-time GeoJSON/ATOM/other feeds. GeoJSON detail links to versioned products including ShakeMap, PAGER and aftershock forecasts when available.

## W18G-D02 — USGS ShakeMap
Primary:
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/shakemap/

ShakeMap provides real-time shaking fields including instrumental intensity, PGA, PGV and spectral acceleration with downloadable GIS/grid products. Product versions/timestamps are archived in ComCat.

## W18G-D03 — USGS PAGER
Primary:
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/pager/

PAGER provides rapid population exposure plus fatality/economic-loss estimates for significant earthquakes. USGS states estimates are generally available within roughly 20–30 minutes for qualifying events and updated as information improves.

## W18G-D04 — USGS Operational Aftershock Forecast
Primary:
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/oaf/
https://earthquake.usgs.gov/data/oaf/schedule.php

USGS forecasts expected/probable aftershocks by magnitude and horizon. Standard automated forecasts begin about 20 minutes after a qualifying mainshock and update frequently early in the sequence.

## W18G-D05 — U.S. Tsunami Warning Centers
Primary:
https://www.tsunami.gov/

NTWC/PTWC expose Atom feeds and digitally signed CAP documents for tsunami products. Public messages include warning/advisory/watch/information state, geographic zones, forecasts, updates and cancellations.

## W18G-D06 — Tsunami Observations
Primary:
https://www.tsunami.gov/?page=productRetrieval

Event products can include forecast arrival/height and later coastal/deep-ocean observed wave measurements.

## W18G-D07 — NOAA/NWS Volcanic Ash Advisories
Primary:
https://www.weather.gov/vaac/
https://www.weather.gov/vaac/VA_advisories

Official VAAC products contain volcano, time, plume/ash observations, altitude/flight levels, forecast movement and advisory updates.

## W18G-D08 — Aviation Weather API / NWS Alerts
Primary:
https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/
https://api.weather.gov/

Use official machine-readable SIGMET/aviation hazard and NWS CAP/JSON-LD products where applicable.

## W18G-D09 — NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center
Primary:
https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/products/alerts-watches-and-warnings
https://services.swpc.noaa.gov/

SWPC provides public watches, warnings and alerts plus machine-readable JSON/text products, Kp/G-scale data and real-time solar-wind/IMF observations.

## W18G-D10 — Space-Weather Mechanism Evidence
Engineering/operational literature documents geomagnetically induced-current risk to power grids, GNSS/HF effects, satellite drag/electronics risk and aviation mitigation.

Use as mechanism evidence only; market value must be independently tested.
