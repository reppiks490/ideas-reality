# Wave 53V — Source Notes

Namespace: **W53V**

## W53V-D01 — USCG Port State Control Detentions
Primary:
https://www.dco.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/Assistant-Commandant-for-Prevention-Policy-CG-5P/Inspections-Compliance-CG-5PC-/Commercial-Vessel-Compliance/Foreign-Offshore-Compliance-Division/Port-State-Control/Detentions/

USCG publishes monthly IMO-reportable safety detention lists with IMO number, vessel, flag and detention report.

## W53V-D02 — Detention Report Content
USCG states detention reports include vessel name, IMO, detention date, type, port, flag, relevant recognized organization and deficiency summary.

Lists can be revised after appeals/validation.

## W53V-D03 — PSIX Bulk Export
Primary:
https://cgmix.uscg.mil/XML/PSIXExportSearch.aspx

Public bulk export provides vessel inspection, operational-control and deficiency data linked by Activity ID.

## W53V-D04 — PSIX Availability Boundary
Primary:
https://cgmix.uscg.mil/PSIX/

PSIX is a weekly FOIA snapshot derived from MISLE and excludes certain unclosed/pending information.

## W53V-D05 — Targeted Flag Administrations
Primary:
USCG Port State Control targeted-flag page.

USCG uses rolling detention ratios and minimum detention counts to assign enhanced targeting.

## W53V-D06 — Targeted Ship Management
Primary:
USCG Targeted Ship Management page.

Managers associated with multiple IMO-reportable detentions within twelve months can receive elevated PSC examination priority.

## W53V-D07 — Vessels Denied U.S. Entry
Primary:
USCG Foreign Vessels Denied Entry Into U.S. Ports page.

USCG can deny entry based on a history of substandard operation in U.S.-jurisdictional waters.

## W53V-D08 — QUALSHIP 21
Primary:
USCG QUALSHIP 21 initiative.

Use enrollment/flag qualification as a separate positive quality state; program criteria were revised effective January 14, 2026.

## W53V-D09 — Flag-State Detentions / Merchant Vessel Data
Primary:
USCG flag-state detention pages and Merchant Vessels of the United States downloads.

Use U.S.-flag detention separately from foreign Port State Control.
