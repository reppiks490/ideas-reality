# Wave 44I — Research Methods

Namespace: **W44I**

## W44I-M01 — Investigation Identity
Use USITC investigation number as primary case key; preserve respondent/product/patent mappings separately.

## W44I-M02 — EDIS Public-Time Ledger
Store document filed time, public-posting availability and ingest time where observable.

## W44I-M03 — Confidentiality Boundary
EDIS contains public and nonpublic/confidential material.

Use only public documents/content.

## W44I-M04 — Institution Is Not Merits
Opening a Section 337 investigation does not imply infringement or future exclusion.

## W44I-M05 — Initial vs Final Determination
ALJ ID and Commission final action are distinct events.

## W44I-M06 — Remedy Scope Parsing
Parse exact product/respondent/order language.

No keyword-only "ban" classification.

## W44I-M07 — Review-Period Access
Model presidential review and bonded entry separately from final exclusion.

## W44I-M08 — Bond Economics
Translate bond to economics only with correct entered-value/product margin assumptions.

## W44I-M09 — Settlement Confidentiality
Public termination may confirm settlement without exposing economic license terms.

Do not invent royalties.

## W44I-M10 — Redesign Evidence Ladder
Company announcement < public agency finding/ruling < realized import/sales evidence.

## W44I-M11 — Product Exposure Mapping
Require sourced U.S. revenue/import exposure and product scope.

## W44I-M12 — Inventory Buffer
Estimate pre-existing U.S. inventory separately from continued import capacity.

## W44I-M13 — Patent Expiry / Validity State
Track asserted patent status and expiration where public; later invalidation can affect order viability.

## W44I-M14 — Appeal Is Not Automatic Stay
Do not assume Federal Circuit appeal suspends USITC remedy absent public legal relief.

## W44I-M15 — General vs Limited Exclusion
Never extrapolate a limited order to the whole product category.

## W44I-M16 — Related-Case Deduplication
District court, PTAB, Federal Circuit and ITC actions can concern same patents/products; parent them without double counting.

## W44I-M17 — Physical Import Validation
Before market testing validate legal state against actual import/sales/product availability where possible.

## W44I-M18 — Promotion Gate
Before equity alpha require OOS improvement in:
remedy probability,
final exclusion,
redesign success,
import availability,
or affected sales.
