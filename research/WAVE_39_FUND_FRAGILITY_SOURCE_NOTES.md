## W39F-D02 — N-PORT Dataset Readme
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/files/nport_readme.pdf

Public tables include fund-reported information, monthly returns/flows, holdings, repo, derivative counterparties/instruments, and securities-lending data.

## W39F-D03 — Form N-PORT
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/files/formn-port.pdf

The form defines holdings, interest-rate risk, securities lending, monthly returns, sales/redemptions, derivatives and other portfolio information.

## W39F-D04 — 2024 N-PORT Amendments
SEC adopted monthly filing/publication enhancements in 2024, but those changes were subsequently delayed.

## W39F-D05 — 2025 Delay
Primary:
SEC April 16, 2025 extension.

Compliance for the 2024 N-PORT amendments was delayed to Nov. 17, 2027 for larger groups and May 18, 2028 for smaller groups.

## W39F-D06 — 2026 N-PORT Proposal
Primary:
https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2026/02/s7-2026-05

In Feb. 2026 SEC proposed further changes including a 45-day monthly filing deadline and restoration of quarterly public publication. Treat proposal as non-effective until adopted.

## W39F-D10 — Evidence Literature
Recent fund-fragility research uses portfolio overlap, common flows and asset liquidity to model fire-sale contagion; derivatives-collateral research shows margin calls can create liquidity shortfalls.

Use as mechanism evidence, not as proof of tradable alpha.
