# Wave 57L — Source Notes

Namespace: **W57L**

## W57L-D01 — FERC eLibrary
Primary:
https://elibrary.ferc.gov/

FERC dockets expose project filings and delegated orders, including granular permissions to commission equipment and introduce hazardous fluids.

## W57L-D02 — FERC LNG Terminal Status
Primary:
https://www.ferc.gov/natural-gas/lng

FERC publishes U.S. LNG terminal maps/status information for operating, approved, under-construction and proposed facilities.

## W57L-D03 — FERC Commissioning Example
Plaquemines LNG docket CP17-66 includes delegated orders authorizing commissioning of individual liquefaction blocks and introduction of hazardous fluids.

Use exact docket issuance time.

## W57L-D04 — DOE LNG Export Authorizations
Primary:
https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/listing-doe-authorizationsordersnotices-issued-2026

DOE/HGEO publishes LNG import/export authorization orders and amendments by docket/date.

## W57L-D05 — DOE Long-Term Contract Information
Primary:
https://www.energy.gov/hgeo/articles/long-term-contract-information-and-registrations

DOE publishes facility-level long-term LNG delivery contracts, natural-gas supply contracts and registrants for facilities with applicable export authorization.

## W57L-D06 — EIA Startup-Timing Analysis
Primary:
https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=64884

EIA explicitly modeled earlier/later LNG facility startup timing and linked it to feedgas demand, storage and natural-gas prices.

## W57L-D07 — EIA LNG Capacity / STEO
Primary:
https://www.eia.gov/naturalgas/
https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/

Use EIA as slower structural/forecast validation, not the earliest commissioning clock.

## W57L-D08 — Operator Milestones
Public operator releases can identify:
first LNG,
first cargo,
substantial completion,
commercial operation,
train turnover.

Examples include Cheniere Corpus Christi Stage 3.
